### Interpretation

- The tile the user sees is a property of every ×2 head so far, not of the GAN: all three put 20 –
  30 % more first difference on the token borders than inside, where the chain and bicubic sit at
  1.0. The head writes each 32 × 32 token from that token's feature and latent patch, so nothing
  ties two tokens' outputs at their border except the trunk's convolutions.
- The DINO swap moved the fixed pattern from 16 px to 32 px; the GAN raised the 32-px part again on
  kotatsu and manga_action. The seam ratio moved less than the stamp.
- `x2dll` is the closest to the chain in DINO distance and has no colour cast on mono, so it is the
  ×2 regression base on these reads; whether `x2dgf512` or `x2dll` ships is the user's read of the
  sheets.
