### Interpretation

- The user's read holds in a narrower form: the reuse is in about 5 % of the textured tiles, almost
  all on halftone, and the set is one or two tiles rather than sixteen. Elsewhere the heads' tiles
  are no more repetitive than the VAE's.
- The repeated tile came with the DINO term, not with the GAN or the init: `x2bll` writes a
  varying weave at the same places and `x2dll` a fixed one, and from one init the share rises with
  the term and its weight (LPIPS 0.45, DINO 54.9 0.55, DINO 245.2 0.76), as does the count of
  departed tiles. DINO's patch tokens are matched per 16-px patch after L2 normalisation, a target
  that a texture of the right statistics at any phase satisfies; with no source of randomness the
  head's cheapest such texture is a fixed function of the place inside the token. LPIPS at 2,000
  rollouts already has a part of it, so the term sets how much, not whether.
- On real originals the B-only heads rarely depart; the held-out set has little halftone, so the
  end sheets and the dB / DINO evals do not see this. The GAN finish makes it appear there, on
  either chain.
- The GAN finish kept that tile, doubled the tiles that half-depart, and added a speck at the
  token centre; the foliage net on castle is its own.
- The discriminator separates every token for the whole finish and the margin does not come down,
  so L_G sits on softplus's linear part: each token gets the same push whatever the head writes
  there. That fits the user's read for what the GAN added (the speck, the net, the half-departed
  tiles); the fixed tile on halftone is in `x2dll`, before any discriminator. The pairing makes
  the separation structural (README, GAN term: PE's linear probe 0.999 paired, 0.88 unpaired), so
  a slower discriminator or a random choice of tapped block is not expected to move the margin.
- The seam ratio is as high on the agreeing tiles as on the departed ones, so the seam and the
  fixed tile are two things: a seam fix leaves the tile, and noise injection (`x2_plan.md`) is the
  proposal that addresses the tile.
