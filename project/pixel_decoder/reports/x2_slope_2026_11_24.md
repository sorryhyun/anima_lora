## The reference code against this GAN term: Interpretation

- Claude's reading before the slope arms (not measured): the slope term here was 25,000
  times lighter than the reference's, so the discriminator was free to separate on sub-level
  high-frequency content, which is the scale of the pattern on flat colour (0.3 – 0.5 levels)
  and of the grain; the saturated margin fits it.
- The reference decoder has no convolution at pixel resolution: a convolution tail is not
  what the reference relies on.
