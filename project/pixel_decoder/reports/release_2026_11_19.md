## Interpretation

On turbo the VAE swap is 15 % of DiT + decode and the head under 1 %: about 1.17× images per
second with text encoding excluded, 1.13× with it. On Spectrum the decode is 3 % of an image either
way. The card's turbo estimate of ~20 % from `promotion_maybe.md` lands at 15 %.

Spectrum's 33.02 median against turbo's 35.40 matches the training reads (base below turbo).
The head time doubles on the Spectrum run (14 ms vs 7 ms); the CFG combine adds two feature-sized
ops and the compiled head may have fallen back to eager after its recompile limit (open item in
`plan.md`); not separated.

v3 and v4 read about 3 dB under the turbo the head was trained on, above the base-head-on-turbo
level (13.7) and near the base-render reads (~33). The head carries over to the current Hub turbo
with a loss; a turbo point trained on v3 / v4 (the `--points … :adapter` recipe, ~7 min per 500
rollouts) is the direct fix.
