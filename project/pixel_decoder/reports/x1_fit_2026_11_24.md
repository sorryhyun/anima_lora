## Interpretation

- By the config's rule (`x1` reaches VAE(x̂0) on the fit windows → the form can, the blur is the
  loss's and the data's): the released form passes the VAE by 19 dB on windows it has fitted, so
  the 8-px spacing and the 8 × 8 linear output are not what merges fine strokes.
  `reports/x1_recipe_2026_11_22.md` had left the form as the suspect; this takes it off.
- The score and the two new meters behave as intended: the picked windows are fine detail, and
  amp and hp_corr put the VAE above the init on every one.
- On these windows the VAE decode itself is 16.8 – 30.4 dB from the original, so the target the
  ×1 base points already use (the original) holds detail the VAE does not return.
- The probe says what the form can write, not what the input determines: 16 windows can be
  memorised from their features. Whether a head can pass the VAE on images it has not seen is a
  question for a trained arm read on held-out pages.
- `deep8` over `x1` by 2.4 dB and `fine4` under it by 1.5 dB at one seed with no spread: not read
  as a ranking.

### Interpretation

- By the config's rule this is its first outcome inside the trained range and its second below
  it. From σ 0.1364 to 0.0577 VAE(x̂0) and `x1dll` rise together (2.0 – 3.1 dB and 1.4 – 2.7 dB on
  the two fine-stroke windows), and the head stays within 0.6 dB of VAE(x̂0). Below 0.0577
  VAE(x̂0) goes on to VAE(z0) while `x1dll` peaks near σ 0.03 and falls; those σ are outside
  what it was trained on, so that says nothing about a head trained there.
- At the σ the generated read uses (0.1), `x1dll` is 3.1 / 5.1 dB under VAE(z0) on the two
  windows and VAE(x̂0) is 3.5 / 5.2 dB under it: the whole of the head's gap to the VAE round
  trip is the decode point's, and the head already returns what the VAE returns from the same
  forward.
- On an image being generated the last latent is x̂0 of the last forward, so the VAE chain has
  the same loss there. A lower last σ (one more forward at about 0.03) is the lever for both;
  on these windows `x1dll` reads 1.2 / 2.5 dB higher at 0.03 than at 0.1 without having been
  trained there.
- Where the VAE round trip itself fails (the weave of 6378235 `win0`, hp_corr 0.40), the head
  reads above VAE(x̂0) in dB and hp_corr and not above VAE(z0): with the original as target it
  is not held to the VAE's decode, and what it can add is bounded by what the forward holds.
- The GAN finish sits 0.8 – 1.6 dB under `x1dll` at every σ with a lower hp_corr: no decode
  point moves the detail it adds onto the original's strokes.
