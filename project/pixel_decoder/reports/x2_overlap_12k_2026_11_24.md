## Interpretation

- Trained from the ×1 init, the overlapping layer ends with part of the border step that the
  converted layer does not have: the token border at × 1.19 sits between `x2ov`'s 0.98 and
  `x2dll`'s 1.35, and the mean tile's peak moves onto the token's last column. The converted
  layer starts as the 8 × 8 output with its edge pixels repeated, which may be what holds the
  windowed sum flat; a fresh layer has no such start.
- Held out, the two heads are 0.03 – 0.07 dB apart with the same DINO distance, and the curve is
  flat by 11,000: the 12,000 rollouts bought nothing over the 2,000-rollout conversion that these
  numbers show.
- By the config's rule (the steps back → `x2ov` stays the parent), `x2ov` stays the parent of the
  GAN finish, pending the user's read of the pair sheets.
