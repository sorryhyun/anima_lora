## Interpretation

- The head reads the int8 DiT at the bf16 level on both decode points; the mono gap on base
  (−0.30 over 6 images) sits inside what a change of page content gives and cannot be separated
  from it with one run per base. The "int8 untested" limit in `docs/anima/inference/pixel_head.md`
  is replaced by these numbers.
- The cond-only feature decodes the CFG trajectory's final image as well as the CFG-combined
  feature does. The difference grows with the last-step σ, where the uncond forward contributes
  more to the final Euler step. Dropping the uncond forward at the last step would save 1 of 2N
  forwards (1.8 % at 28 steps); it is not wired, and at 20 steps it costs 0.08 dB on every image.
