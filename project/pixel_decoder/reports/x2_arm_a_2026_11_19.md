## Interpretation

- The second teacher draw (42 – 46) is not the level this head can be read against. The teacher
  on the head's own decode already sits at 33.7 / 29.6 / 28.2 from the teacher on VAE(x̂0): the
  ×1 decode's error (37.4 / 32.7 / 32.8 against VAE(x̂0)) grows through the teacher. The design's
  premise, "the target is a function of the input", held for the teacher's noise and missed
  this: the target is a function of VAE(x̂0), which the head knows only to its decode error.
- The washed iris and smeared glyphs come from the ×1 decode, since the chain shows them; they
  belong to the "pixel losses on the head" topic, not to the ×2 stage.
- What the ×2 stage itself is missing is edge sharpness: it reads 1.7 – 3.0 dB under the chain
  and soft where the chain is crisp.
- The stage can express the teacher's output (2 windows: `wide` within 0.5 dB of a second draw at
  base), so the form is not the limit. On 16 windows, where the ×2 pixels outnumber the
  parameters about 4 to 1 and the stage has to learn the mapping, every trainable arm stops at
  32.4 – 33.3, six times the parameters buy 0.7 – 0.9 dB, and more blocks buy nothing. A small
  regression student of a 119M SR model lands on soft edges; that is the usual case in SR
  distillation, where a perceptual or adversarial term carries the sharpness.
- The frozen base stays under bicubic, so a ×2 stage beside an untouched release head is not
  available in this form; and with the base trainable the ×1 output lost about 1 dB in 1,000
  rollouts.
- Not separated: slow convergence against a ceiling on 16 windows (the cosine schedule ends as
  the curve flattens); the 2-window numbers rest on one window per point.

## The design as written before the run

Moved from `x2_plan.md` on 2026-11-20.

Parent `mixr_1119_1455` (points `0.0577-0.1364 0.5:adapter`, pages 0.33,
replay 64 × 16 updates, LR 2e-4, fp32 stream). Changed together: the second 2× stage; its
target, `rsd_student_18k` at the ComfyUI node's defaults on VAE(x̂0), recipe loss; windowed
training, one 30 × 30-token window per rollout with the loss on the inner 24 × 24 away from image
edges and the teacher run on the window only. Question: does the ×2 output read level with
VAE → ResShift ×2? Level → promote the stage and read arm B against it; soft → capacity before
arm B; ×1 under the parent → a frozen base. Bars: spread unknown. Smoke `smoke_x2` gave the
reference levels (bicubic 31.86 / 28.25, second draw 46.65 / 40.36, 2 test / 8 text images).

Outcome: soft, and ×1 under the parent; the capacity and frozen-base branches were both read by
the fit probes (above). No user verdict was asked on arm A's sheets beyond the tone read.
