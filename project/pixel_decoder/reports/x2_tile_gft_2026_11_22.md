- Read, set before the run: the evals at 250 … 1000 beside the micro arms' rows; the `text_04`
  place beside `x2bgan2`; the card's monochrome and colour renders (base 28 steps, CFG 4, seed

  Bars: spread unknown.
- Cost: 25 – 30 minutes.

#### Interpretation

- Question: does the ratio-2 term on the converged head give the shipped file's lines without
  the tile? Yes → this head replaces the Hub file on the user's read. Tile still there → the
  schedule is not the cause, next is a noise input on the ×2 stage. No tile, softer lines → ratio
  and length on this form.
- The tile appears only in the run where the term came on at rollout 1000: the head was at its
  highest LPIPS (0.27), the LR at 0.85 of its peak, and the term's weight at 0.151, ten times its
  later level. The reading: the head learned there to answer "fine texture" with a fixed motif,
  and kept it.
- The 16-px pitch is the grid's: where neighbouring latent pixels carry the same feature, a
  deterministic upsampler can only emit a pattern with the latent pixel's period. A texture finer
  than that whose phase the feature does not carry comes out as a tile or as nothing.
- The whole-image lag-16 number does not separate the tiled run from the tile-free ratio-4 run,
  so no number stands for the tile; it is read on crops.

#### Interpretation

- The pinwheel tile does not appear when the same ratio-2 term is put on the converged head: the
  schedule of `x2bgan2`, not the ratio, is where it came from. One run each way; the seed twin
  `x2gftw_1121_2300` reads whether that holds on a second seed.
- On screentone `x2gft` still writes a regular pattern (the diagonal weave), milder than the
  tile. Whether it reads as screentone is the user's call.
- The fine-stroke blur is already in the ×1 head and in `x2b4k`, so it is the shared trunk's, not the
  second stage's: the VAE decodes the strokes from the same latent, so the features hold them
  and a regression head averages them. The GAN term paints a texture over the soft area (a hatch
  in the shipped file, irregular strokes in `x2gft`) and does not recover the strokes. The lever
  is the trunk; the ×1 batch reads whether length moves it, and the `mixr` decode in
  `20261121-2300-x1night_renders` reads whether the VAE(x̂0) target kept the strokes that the
  real-image target lost.
