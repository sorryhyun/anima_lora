## Interpretation

- The hidden layer carries a strong code of the position inside the token, in the B head as
  much as in the GAN finishes (more, by the top λ). It is how a position writes its own part of
  the token, and its mean part cancels what `out2.bias` writes alone (2.04 levels down to 0.09).
  Removing those directions removes the cancellation: every `p32` edit raises stamp32, the grain
  and the sky fold. The patterns are what a cancellation leaves over, not energy in directions
  of their own, so no fold that damps directions separates them at `out2`.
- The 8-px grid on flat colour is the one pattern with an exact linear form at this layer, and
  the flat tokens' hidden values lie in four directions. Completing the cancellation on those
  four (`f8_k4`) takes the sky fold to the B head's level at no cost in amp; at 16 directions
  the edit starts to reach textured content (amp −0.025), and past that it removes detail.
- The token tile on fine colour texture (castle s0) is untouched by any edit here. It is a
  joint arrangement of a token's 16 positions chosen by content, which a change of `out2` that
  is the same at every position does not reach.
- What this leaves open for a finish at ratio 1.0 – 2.0 with `f8_k4` on top: the flat-colour grid
  no longer rises with the ratio, so the ratio is bounded by the castle tile alone (0.003 /
  0.013 / 0.054 at 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0).
