  (7.08 both), so the pairing is not what saturates the discriminator. Without the pair the
  discriminator starts lower and is slower for the first 300 rollouts, with a loss three times
  the control's throughout, and separates every token all the same. Open 3 closes.

  on flat colour and the same line breaks. No number reads as a better trade than the control.
- The pattern on flat colour and the token tile are there after 1,000 rollouts of either form,
  so they come with the finish's push as such. The step that addresses flat colour directly is
  the flat-token term (a high-pass penalty where the target is flat) on the control's recipe;
  `x2gf1k_1124_1256` is its control. Not queued.
