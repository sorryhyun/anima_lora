### Interpretation

- By the config's rule the head did not take the noise under this loss: the draw gap peaked at
  0.14 – 0.16 levels and fell to 0.08 – 0.09, and every tile number is level with the control's.
- The difference the user saw between the two heads is about 15 times one head's draw gap, so it
  is the difference of two training paths, not of noise draws.
- Two causes are not separated. A zero-init noise map starts where its expected gradient is zero
  (the output does not depend on the draw), and it shares the finetune's 5e-5; and L1 / MSE with a
  paired discriminator may not reward a random phase at all. The weights rose and then fell, which
  leans to the second.

  cell with the cell borders visible.

### Interpretation

- The boxes are two grids, the 32-px token and the 8-px output patch: `out2` writes each 8 × 8
  patch from one position with no overlap, and nothing ties a patch to its neighbour.
- They are in the B-only heads; the GAN finish raised the grain on flat regions (inner 0.22 →
  0.37) and the patch step with it. Noise inputs do not bear on them.
- A step at a patch border cannot be written by an output that overlaps: 16 × 16 patches at
  stride 8 under a window, summed (`x2_plan.md`, Open).
