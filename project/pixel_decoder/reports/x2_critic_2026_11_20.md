control; the branch is closed on the user's word. Moved from `x2_plan.md`.

## The form and the dry run `smoke_x2dmd_1120_0815`

### Facts

- The form (`bench/pixel_readout/x2_dmd.py::X2Critic`, flags `--lambda_dmd`, `--lambda_gan`,
  `--critic*` on `x2_head.py`): the SR line's RSD loop (`project/finished/sr/distill_rsd/`,
  DESIGN.md Algorithm 1) with the ×2 head as the generator. One random 256² crop of the ×2
  output per update is encoded by a fp32 VQ-f4 encoder (the gradient reaches the head through
  it) into ẑ0; z_y is the latent of the bicubic ×2 of VAE(x̂0) at the same crop, and is also
  the conditioning (the ×2 line's `latent` convention). DMD term: ẑ0 noised on ResShift's
  forward at t ~ U{0…14}, the frozen 15-step ×2 teacher (`resshift_x2_final.pth`, EMA, step
  30,000) and a fake critic (a trainable copy) both predict x0, their difference divided by
  |teacher x0 − ẑ0| is pushed onto ẑ0. GAN term: the SR line's `DiscHead` on the fake critic's
  bottleneck at t = 0, real = the latent of the 1-step teacher's ×2 at the same crop. The
  critic takes one update per head update (AdamW 5e-5, betas 0.9 / 0.95, discriminator loss
  at 3e-3 inside it), on the head update's own crop, detached. No text input anywhere.
- Dry run `smoke_x2dmd_1120_0815` (`x2real_1119_2242`'s command with `--critic --term_log`,
  both weights 0, 100 rollouts, the ×2 stage from its default init; results (local)
  `bench/pixel_readout/results/20261120-0828-smoke_x2dmd_1120_0815/`): peak VRAM 14.8 GB
  over rollouts and updates (reserved 15.1; 9.3 before the critic is built), 6.05 s per
  rollout against `x2real_1119_2242`'s 1.49, critic update 119 ms (eager). Critic at
  rollout 100: `c_fake` 0.186, `d_real` +9.7, `d_fake` −9.1. Unweighted gradient norms over
  the head at rollouts 50 / 100: rec2 0.34 / 0.087, LPIPS 7.7 / 2.8, DMD 5.5 / 3.2, GAN
  114 / 30.5.

### Interpretation

- The loop fits on the 16 GB card with about 1 GB to spare; nothing else can sit on the card
  beside it.
- At 6 s per rollout a 1,000-rollout run from the default init is about 100 minutes. The arm
  therefore continues a trained ×2 head for 250 rollouts, which is also where RSD's loop
  starts (its student begins as a copy of the teacher, not from noise).
- The dry run's gradient norms come from a ×2 stage 100 rollouts old and set no weight.
- The discriminator separates real from fake at once, so a feature-distance probe would not
  tell the critic's bottleneck from PE-Spatial as a discriminator backbone; the PE variant
  (FastFrames' form on the local PE-Spatial-B16) is read as its own micro arm after this one.

## Micro arm `x2dmd_1120_0848` and its control `x2ctl_1120_0848` — design, written before the runs

### Facts

- Sizing smoke `smoke_x2dmdw_1120_0830` (the arm's settings with both weights 0:
  `--resume_x2 x2real_1119_2242 --lr 5e-5 --critic --term_log`, 100 rollouts; results (local)
  `bench/pixel_readout/results/` under that label). Unweighted gradient norms over the head
  at rollouts 50 / 100, one replay sample each: rec2 0.158 / 0.051, LPIPS 14.6 / 1.55, dc
  0.22 / 0.11, DMD 5.85 / 3.16, GAN 146 / 154. Critic at rollout 100: `c_fake` 0.098,
  `d_real` +7.3, `d_fake` −7.4. 5.84 s per rollout, peak 14.8 GB.
- Control: `x2real_1119_2242` continued for 250 rollouts on its own recipe (`--resume_x2`,
  points `0.0577-0.1364:real 0.5:adapter`, pages 0.33, window 24, margin 3, λ_lpips 0.1,
  λ_dc 1.0, λ_lpips_x1 0.05, replay 64 × 16), with `--lr` 2e-4 → 5e-5 (RSD's rate for its
  three nets; a new cosine cycle with the 200-step warmup).
- Arm: the control with `--lambda_dmd 0.1 --lambda_gan 3e-4`, nothing else changed. Both
  runs carry `--term_log`, so they draw the same rollouts, windows and replay samples.
- λ_dmd 0.1 puts the DMD gradient (0.3 – 0.6 weighted) at the weighted LPIPS gradient's
  level (0.16 – 1.46). λ_gan 3e-4 keeps RSD's ratio to the DMD weight (3e-3) and puts the
  GAN gradient (about 0.045 weighted) near a tenth of the DMD's.
- Read: the held-out set of `x2real_1119_2242` (test 12 / text 8 / manga 4, both points),
  two-column crops `control | arm` at the run's own crop windows, labelled, and the run's
  `chain | ×2 head` sheets; then the five renders of `x2bic_1119_2335` (seed 510) through
  `p3_generated.py --x2` for both heads. Numbers beside them: dB, LPIPS and dark lift against
  the teacher, the ×1 output against VAE(x̂0) and the original. The user's read decides.
  Bars: spread unknown.
- Cost: control about 12 minutes, arm about 33 (250 rollouts at 5.8 s).

### Interpretation

- Question: with the two critic terms, do the ×2 output's lines, screentone and dark tone
  move from the control's toward the chain's? Yes → the render read, then a longer run is
  proposed. Soft as the control → a λ sweep upward, or the PE-Spatial discriminator arm.
  Artefacts → λ down, GAN first.
- The two weights rest on two single-sample gradient readings each; the arm logs the same
  norms every 50 rollouts so the drift is on record.
- Changed together and not separated: the DMD term and the GAN term (RSD's package).
