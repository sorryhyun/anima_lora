# P3: the P2 head on generated images and under a LoRA, and where manga pages lose (2026-11-19)

Covers `p3_1119_1056` (generated images, base and a LoRA) and `manga_1119_1052` (real manga
pages, teacher-forced, against the original pixels). Head: `convnext_1119_0938` (no skip, 29.7M,
trained on anima-base real latents at σ 0.0577 against VAE(x0)). Code:
`bench/pixel_readout/p3_generated.py`, `render_sheets.py`; results under `bench/pixel_readout/results/` (local).

### Interpretation

The head carries over to generated images and to the merged15 LoRA: the colour mean is 2 dB under
the real held-out set, inside the seed-to-seed gap of single prompts, and the LoRA is not below
base. The low cases are dense fine strokes, the same axis as screentone, so the open problem is one
content type, not generated images or adapters as such. For a generated image x_final is a fixed
function of the head's two inputs (x_t and the CFG feature), so what the head misses on manga is
not missing information; it is decoding the head has not learned (data) or cannot express at
16-px patches (capacity). The CFG feature is safe to use although the head saw only cond
features.

- Generated manga, teacher-forced (`p3_1119_1056`, base, 6): 21.86 mean against VAE(x_final).
- Claude's read (labelled, sheets 02 ARMS, 08 haruhisky): the VAE keeps lines and balloon text and
  turns fine screentone into a coarser grey pattern; the head keeps panels, faces and large
  glyphs, softens small balloon text (some glyphs misshapen in ARMS) and smooths tone and hatching
  into flat grey.

### Interpretation

On real pages three losses stack. The VAE alone costs down to 21–25 dB on dense-tone pages; the
σ_last noise costs about as much again (VAE(x̂0) 24–36 against 36–38 on colour), and no head
reading σ_last features can exceed that ceiling against VAE(x0); the head then sits 1–6 dB under
VAE(x̂0), comparable to its gap on colour. So restoring the original pixels of dense-tone pages is
out of reach at this tap; restoring what the DiT produces (VAE(x̂0), or VAE(x_final) when sampling)
is the reachable goal, and that is where training data and capacity apply.
