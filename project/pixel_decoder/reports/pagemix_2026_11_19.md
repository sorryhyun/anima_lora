## Interpretation

The head is past `replay_1119_1225` on every set and every image, and the manga set rose without
costing colour, so the mix does not trade one domain for the other. Which of the three changes
(x̂0 target, 2.2× the rollouts, pages) carries the gain is not separated; the x̂0 numbers rising
more than the x0 ones fits the target change doing part of it.

Tone does not move: mean shifts are a fraction of one 8-bit level and contrast holds within about
1 %. The +3 saturation on manga is HSV inflating near-grey pixels; in absolute chroma the head adds
about 0.5 level of tint over the VAE's own on greyscale pages, below what reads on screen. This
covers teacher-forced real pages; the grey tint Claude saw on P3a's generated mono images is not
re-checked by it.

What is left (speed lines, footnote glyphs, screentone) is high-frequency detail at the scale the
head writes 8×8 patches, the same residue P2 and P3a left.
