# piecenat — where run0925_300f actually failed, by layer (2026-09-25)

`next.md` § 4a read run0925_300f as "nothing bought, at any displacement".
That was measured on `native_sent` (full-string), `word` exact and `target`
— and all three turn out to be blind to what the run did buy. The
discriminating cell — **a trained piece alone in a native scene** — had
never been measured (the run's `native` group was only the frozen あ/い
controls). This read fills it.

## Method

The probe `native` ruler with pieces as the chars, on the trained table
(ctx overlay, job `20260925-192222-9cc22b`) and on the seed
(`--seed_only`, job `…-1829d3`); 8 pieces spanning 2/3/5 glyphs, 16
renders per piece per clause (8 prompts × 2 seeds), `en` + `swap` clauses:

```
S="project/cjk_anima_scale/scale.py --run run0925_300f --stage joint0507_0305"
$S --steps eval [--seed_only] --eval_only native --submit --queue \
   --eval_extra --native_chars "しい,った,です,メン,すごい,ちょっと,ありがとう,こんにちは" --eval_tag piecenat
```

Two readings per cell: **lenient** = the full piece string appears in any
box read (sfx or VL), **official** = `hit_sfx ∧ hit_vl`. Outputs under
`rows_scale_joint0507_0305_run0925_300f_{ctx,seed}/native_piecenat/`.

## The other two layers, re-read from the existing 4a eval

- **Flat/bubble identity (`word`, 18 pieces × 2)**: exact 4/36, but the
  piece string is **contained in the read 18/36**. The dominant miss is
  doubling (すごい → すごいい, こと → ここと, メン → メンン, 私の →

- **Sentences (`native_sent`)**: the trained pieces render *inside* the
  sentence reads — った 13/16, しい 11/16 present — while the full
  strings sit at 0 (やったネ 0/16 full). ちょっと 1/16 and こんにちは
  0/16: the long pieces fail everywhere.

## Read

run0925_300f's failure list is **(i) doubling** (kills every exact
ruler), **(ii) sentence assembly** (pieces present, strings not), and
**(iii) 3+-glyph pieces** (at floor alone and in sentences; こんにちは —
one 5-glyph row — bought nothing at ~90 draws/row). **2-glyph piece
identity + native trigger was bought**, on the very mix § 4a blamed.

Consequences:

- § 4a's "piece displacement on this mix buys no word" stands only for
  the *acceptance-axis* rulers; "nothing bought" is corrected in
  `next.md` § 4b. The sentence/short/grid-share suspicion now attaches to
  doubling/assembly, not to glyph identity.
- **Rule**: `word` exact, `native_sent` and `target` cannot tell a dead
  piece run from one that bought piece natives — read piecenat (the
  `native` ruler with piece chars, seed floor beside it) before calling
  a piece run dead.
