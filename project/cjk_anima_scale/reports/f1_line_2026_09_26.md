# f1_line — Stage F1: a factorized donor, rows + a gated v_line (2026-09-26)

`../proposal_factorizedrows.md` § 3. Stage B's donor is retrained on its own
data (36 donors, 568 words, count tier 0.3; same seed rows, trainer and
90 steps / row). Beside the rows `r_i` it trains one `v_line`
(`ExtDelta.line`, zero-init), which the hook adds only to a pack row with a
pack neighbour in the T5 ids (a spelled word). F0 had put the gate there.
**Verdict: F1 fails its main criterion, and the fall-back branch of § 3
applies.** Gradient moves the shared direction `u_S` out of the rows (its
energy share 24 % → 5 %, into `v_line`), and with it the same-glyph repeats
alone (50 → 35 / 144, floor 29). **The line layout stays in `r_i`,
though.** Gate off, a donor single alone still renders a line of other
glyphs in 94 / 144 renders (floor 47, plain donor 102), and official stays
at 50 (floor 91, plain 43). **`v_line` itself is a working learned line
mode.** On the seed rows of the held-out 10 it composes the held-out words
like the post-hoc `u_S` (≤ 1 edit 64 vs 66 / 160), with no extraction step
and singles at the floor through the gate. It is about 2× `u_S`'s dose,
though, and in-word doubling (B) rises with it (`dup` 77 vs 44). One job,
`20260926-193243-8773e0` (train 30 min + 3 reads 41 min).

- `v_line`: norm 204, cos **0.79** to `u_S`, projection 162 on it. That is

  anyway (64, where `u_S` at α 2 fell to 34), so `v_line` is not just a
  larger `u_S`. Collapsing doubled glyphs lifts the words to 78: half the
  word misses are in-word doubling.

## 4. What it decides

- **§ 3's second branch: the line behaviour stays in `r_i` with `v_line`
  available.** Gate on / off exposure moved the shared direction but not
  the layout. This does not close the parameterization. The known hole is
  the data: the count tier covers 24–40 px only, and b0305's lines
  (≈ 18 px) have no alone counterpart. F1b (alone items at the b0305 px)
  needs a band-law row first.
- **Shipping the mode does not need `r_i` to be clean.** Seed rows +
  `v_line` + the gate already give u1-level composition with the singles
  at the floor. The one thing worse than post-hoc `u_S` is in-word
  doubling, and `v_line` sits at about 2× `u_S`'s dose. A `v_line` dose
  read at 0.5 (training-free, the held-out keys, ≈ 25 min) tests whether
  B falls back to u1's 44 while composition holds.
- Stage I (§ 4) now runs against a mode that exists: `v_line` from here.
  The identity question it asks is the one this read left open. Every row
  trained on these recipes renders a line when alone.

## 5. `v_line` dose 0.5 (the `dose` leg, job `20260926-205754-a14db2`)

`tf_l1_line0.5` is the seed rows + 0.5 · `v_line` (projection on `u_S`
≈ 81, about u1's dose), read on the held-out keys (en + swap). Envelope:
`experiments/f1_line/results/20260926-2057-l1/result.json`.

| | floor | u1 (`u_S`) | `v_line` 1 | **`v_line` 0.5** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| words ≤ 1 edit / 160 | 11 | 66 | 64 | **80** |
| words ≤ 2 edits | 93 | 113 | 105 | **131** |
| words official | 0 | 12 | 14 | **17** |
| words `dup` | 26 | 44 | 77 | **54** |
| words `le1c` | 11 | 69 | 78 | **89** |
| singles official / 320 | 149 | 109 | 152 | **152** |
| singles repeat | 25 | 53 | 24 | **26** |

- vs u1: ≤ 1 edit 37 / 23 (p 0.09), ≤ 2 edits 34 / 16 (p 0.015), `dup`
  27 / 17 (p 0.17), singles repeat 9 / 36 (p 7e-5), singles official
  68 / 20 (p 3e-7). vs floor, ≤ 1 edit 75 / 6.
- **Half the dose is the operating point, and it beats the post-hoc
  `u_S` on every word read.** In-word `dup` falls from 77 to 54, within
  noise of u1's 44 (p 0.17) and still +28 over the floor. The gate keeps
  the singles at the floor.
- Per word (≤ 1 edit, en · swap): ひまわり 7 · 12, さくら 4 · 0, みどり 9 · 8,
  くもり 9 · 9, まくら 11 · 11. さくら (swap) is the one word the lower
  dose lost (v1 5 → 0). みどり, which `v_line` 1 broke (0 · 2), recovers.
- **Where this leaves the line mode:** seed rows + 0.5 · `v_line` + the
  run gate is the best composition read on held-out kana so far, with no
  per-row training and no extraction. In-word doubling (B) is the open
  cost; no dose tried separates it from composition.
