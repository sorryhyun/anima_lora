# stage_b — a singles donor, held-out kana: the line mode transfers, doubling with it (2026-09-26)

`../proposal.md` § 2. Stage A (`transplant_piece_2026_09_26.md`) moved a
piece direction out of sample. This is the singles version. 36 donor kana
are trained on in-line words with a count tier. Their shared Δ direction
`u_S` is added at one coefficient to the seed rows of 10 kana that no donor
word contains, and read on 5 spelled words made only of those 10.
**Verdict: composition transfers out of sample, and doubling travels with
it. On the held-out words ≤ 1 edit goes 11 → 66 / 160 (random ⟂ control 9;
u1 vs random 60 / 3, p 9e-15), and exact hits go 0 → 12. On the held-out
singles, repeats go 25 → 53 / 320 (p 6e-5) and official goes 149 → 109. The
count tier at weight 0.3 did not separate the two, in the donor's own rows
or in the transplant. The proposal § 2 rule passes on composition and fails
on "repeats at floor".** Jobs `20260926-155507-64a8f8` (train + donor read
+ floor render) and `20260926-165905-77019b` (transplant). 16 renders per
cell, one seed per arm.

Script: `experiments/stage_b/run_exp.py`. Envelopes in
`experiments/stage_b/results/`: `…-1554-data1` (data), `…-1555-gpu1`
(train, donor read, floor), `…-1658-t1` (build: direction + arms),
`…-1659-t1` (transplant read).

## 1. Design

- **Held out**: ひ ま わ り さ く ら み ど も. Words: ひまわり さくら
  みどり くもり まくら (each made only of the ten, no glyph repeated inside
  a word).
- **Donors** (36): the most frequent hiragana after the held-out ten, in
  the pure-hiragana lines of the manga109s dialogue pool:
  `いなんかあうしたこだそれでよはおとふすてにるやえつがねきめろけちせばほご`.
  The frequency order put の at 18th. It was swapped for the 37th, ふ: の and
  を are the two kana whose space-prefixed form is a different Qwen token
  (`" の"` → row 1296, not の's 31), so a spelled caption cannot address
  their single row.
- **Donor words** (568): those corpus lines of 2–6 glyphs, after stripping
  edge punctuation, that are all donor glyphs with no glyph repeated. A
  repeated glyph in training would itself teach doubling. Lines that
  contain any 3-glyph substring of こんにちは are also dropped. Every
  donor is in ≥ 5 words (せ 5, median 47). A draw picks a donor glyph
  uniformly, then one of its words.
- **こんにちは** is made only of donor glyphs and trigram-held-out from the
  words. It is the donor's own held-out composition read, and its floor was
  already cached.
- **Table**: spell_b's `scene_spelled` (unspaced image, spaced caption) in
  the two piece-band groups, plus a **count tier** `scene_single_small`.
  The count tier is one donor glyph alone at 28–40 font px, in a bubble it
  fills 0.2–0.4 of (its one-glyph fit), captioned alone, at weight 0.3 of
  b0507. It covers only 24–40 px ink: a single under 24 px has no window
  in the band law, so b0305's ≈ 18 px lines have no count counterpart.
  2 400 items (b0507: spelled 840 + count 360, ink px 35 / 33; b0305:
  spelled 1 200, 18 px), about 10 count items per glyph.
- **Trainer**: the fixed one, 90 steps / row → 3 240 steps × batch 4,
  23.6 min. End `warm_cos` 0.82, `warm_drift` 0.59 (spell_b 0.31). Per-row
  rel Δ 0.46–0.69.

## 3. The direction (CPU)

- `u_S` = the normalized mean of the 36 donors' Δ vs the seed, each with
  its component along its own seed row removed. Split-half cos (even vs
  odd donors) **0.82**; Stage A's 292 pieces gave 0.97. `u_S` holds
  24.4 % of the donors' Δ energy. The donors' pairwise cos is 0.22
  (spell_b's five 0.19).
- Step = the donors' mean projection: **75.2** (sd 15.7; か 40 … う 112).
  That is 26–32 % of a held-out seed row's norm (239–293). Row norm after
  the move: ×1.03–1.08 for u1, ×1.04–1.06 for the random ⟂ control at the
  same step.

## 5. Read

- **The singles' line mode is a transferable direction.** Estimated on 36
  donor rows and applied at one coefficient to 10 rows that never trained,
  it composes every held-out word. The 4-glyph ひまわり goes 0 → 14 / 32
  within one edit. The norm-matched random direction does nothing to the
  words (9 vs floor 11). This is the out-of-sample read that
  `transplant_line` § 4 could not give, and it is stronger than Stage A's
  piece read, where official was n.s.
- **Doubling rides the same direction.** Every held-out single but り
  repeats at least as often under u1 as at the floor, and official drops on
  the glyphs the seed rendered well (さ 15 → 6, ど 18 → 10, み 19 → 11). The
  random step of the same size leaves singles at the floor (official 157,
  repeat 32, n.s.). So the loss is the direction's, not the norm's.
- **The count tier as run does not split them.** It did not hold count in
  the donor's own rows (§ 2), and the direction fit on those rows carries
  doubling to the held-out ones. At this dose (weight 0.3, ≈ 10 items /
  glyph, 24–40 px only) the count signal either did not enter the rows or
  entered along `u_S` itself. That weakens H3 (proposal § 3.2), but it
  does not close it: a heavier count tier, a count tier at the b0305 px,
  or B2's 0.7–0.9 canvas are all unrun.
- **What the proposal asked** (§ 2 decision): transplant ≥ the shared-only
  in-sample level with repeats at floor → the adaptation route is real.
  The first half passes by a wide margin; the second fails. So the route is
  real for composition, and it ships doubling unless doubling is bounded
  first. That makes the proposal's § 3 doubling track the next work, ahead
  of transplant + post-train.

## 7. The α sweep (proposal 3.3 a, 2026-09-26)

Job `20260926-181732-f16005`, envelope `…-1817-t1` (u1 read back from disk,
no random arm). `dup` = a word's read holds a doubled glyph; `≤ 1c` = ≤ 1
edit once doubled glyphs are collapsed (both added to `run_exp.py` the
same day, so the old arms were re-scored). Step 75 × α.

| α | words ≤ 1 | `dup` | ≤ 1c | exact | singles official · repeat | singles kana |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| floor | 11 | 26 | 11 | 0 | 149 · 25 | 299 |
| 0.5 | 40 | 49 | 43 | 2 | 139 · 36 | 306 |
| **1** | **66** | **44** | **69** | **12** | 109 · 53 | 304 |
| 2 | 34 | **73** | 44 | 5 | **49** · 60 | 276 |

(words / 160, singles / 320, en + swap.) u2 vs floor: official 12 / 107
(p 3e-20), `dup` 59 / 12 (p 1e-8), kana 14 / 38.

- **Composition peaks at α 1** (inverted U: 40 → 66 → 34), and exact peaks
  with it (2 → 12 → 5).
- **In-word doubling is not a monotone function of the dose.** It jumps to
  +20 over the floor already at α 0.5, is flat to α 1 (49 → 44), and
  climbs at α 2 (73), where collapsing buys back 10 of the ≤ 1 loss
  (34 → 44). No α keeps `dup` at the floor while composition gains, and
  α 1 is the dosed arm with the least `dup`. A per-row cap (proposal
  § 3.4 b) can pick α 1 but cannot take in-word doubling below +18.
- **The singles alone degrade monotonically** (repeat 25 → 36 → 53 → 60,
  official 149 → 139 → 109 → 49). At α 2 kana itself falls (299 → 276,
  contained 280 → 197), the start of a wipe. Nowhere does composition
  gain while the singles stay at the floor, so for a glyph alone the line
  mode is one thing (H2-like), and the gate (§ 3.4 (0)) is the only bound
  on record.
- A reading, not a verdict: in-word `dup` at α 0.5 ≈ α 1 while ≤ 1 edit
  rises by 26 fits "the line is switched on before the sequence is
  resolved". Doubling then fills the line with the glyphs it has, and it
  falls only as composition gets stronger. At α 2 the overdose case
  returns.
