# grid_box — the grid item's cells as its loss box (2026-09-25)

Until this read every grid / flat item trained under the plain canvas-mean
MSE: `train.py` and `conflict.py` gave the box-share form to `src == "scene"`
only (the probe's `train/stage.py` rule, carried over). A 24–32 px cell is
0.3–0.8 % of a 448–512 canvas, so a piece row in a `grid_string` cell
received about a hundredth of the loss a `scene_piece` row did — the
10–60 × per-draw gap of `conflict_joint_2026_09_25.md`'s price table was the
loss form, not the layout. `[train].grid_box = 1` (default since this date,
every stage file and `config._TRAIN_DEFAULTS`) makes a grid item's box the
**union of its cell boxes**, share from the joined text's glyph count (a
2×2 of 2-glyph pieces is 8 glyphs → the 0.5 cap; a cell then carries
≈ s / k). One mask per item, shared by every row in it: a row learns from its
own cell because the position clause binds it there, not because the loss
pairs them. A flat 1×1 stays plain (its box is 10–60 % of the canvas anyway,
and flat draws hold `swap` / frame independence — untouched on purpose).
`--grid_box 0` on `scale.py` reproduces every arm before this date.

Code: `cjk_scale/loss.py` (`item_boxes`, `box_mask`, the `grid_box` arg),
`train.py` / `conflict.py` (the `src == "grid"` gate), `scale.py --grid_box`
(train and conflict), `tests/test_line.py::test_grid_box_union_mask`.

Effective per-cell loss weight, `b30` data (median box / canvas):

| recipe (stage) | cell px | cells | plain | union share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| grid_single 2×2–3×3 (0709) | 94 | 6 | 2.9 % | 0.5 / 6 ≈ 8 % |
| grid_single (0507) | 27 | 6 | 0.29 % | ≈ 8 % |
| grid_string (0507) | 29 | 6 | 0.66 % | ≈ 8 % |
| grid_string (0305) | 12–24 | 6 | ≈ 0.3 % | ≈ 8 % |
| scene_single / scene_piece | 52 / 38 | 1 | 1.1 / 1.3 % of canvas, but s = 25 / 33 % | – |

## 3. The single price table — nothing moved

`conflict_run0923_micro_b30_single_plain` (`110647-dbfb52`) vs `…_single_gbox`
(`110639-ee0569`), recipes `scene_single` + `grid_single`, 0709 + 0507.

| stage | recipe | plain | gbox |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0709 | grid_single (94 px cells + the 1×1 flat) | 0.0057 | 0.0058 |
| 0709 | scene_single | 0.030 | 0.030 |
| 0507 | grid_single (27 px cells) | 0.0105 | 0.0100 |
| 0507 | scene_single | 0.031 | 0.034 |

The mask fires on these items (6 cells, 3 % of the canvas, checked on the
`b30` records) and the 0507 cell's weight went 0.29 % → 8 %, yet the single
rows' per-draw gradient is unchanged (‖ḡ‖ 0.061 → 0.059, coh 0.17, half
0.69–0.70). The in-box residual of a **warm** single at the seed (24 / 32,
norm 1.1–1.8) is ≈ 0 — the row already draws its glyph, so there is no
in-box error to weight; what it gets from a grid_single item is a consistent
(half +0.7) out-of-box pull that the union share does not touch. The pieces
respond because they are half-trained (norm 0.6–0.8, 2 / 16): the in-box
residual is the doubling error itself. **The union share amplifies an in-box
residual; it buys nothing where there is none.** Consequences: the trained
pair's single-side differences are noise (§ 1); for *cold* singles (new
kanji rows, where the exposure budget lives) the same lever is unread and is
the one place a small-cell `grid_single` could still pay — `windows.py`'s
0507 small-single row stays ceiling-only.

## 4. The direction probe — `grid_string` alone (job `20260925-115621-d27f50`, 15.6 min)

`conflict_run0923_micro_b30_gridstr_dir`: the § 2 argv with
`--conflict_recipes grid_string`, so the stage pairs are the recipe's own.
Split-half reliability is 0.92–0.99 on every row and band (grid draws are
the cleanest gradient on record), so a pair cosine is read against ≈ 0.95.

| pair | cos / keep (8 pieces) | per row |
|---|---|---|
| 0507 (0.5–0.7) × 0305 (0.3–0.5) | **+0.91 / 0.98** | 0.88–0.97, every row |
| 0507 × 0.7–0.9 | **+0.27 / 0.81** | 0.03–0.42; すご 0.03, メン 0.10 |
| 0.7–0.9 × 0305 | +0.31 / 0.82 | 0.06–0.38 |

And against what the chain's 0507 stage actually moved the row by:
Δ0507 · −ḡ is +0.27 for 0507's own band, +0.26 for 0.3–0.5, **+0.06 for
0.7–0.9** (per row −0.02 … +0.11).

**Verdict.** 0.3–0.7 is one pull on a grid piece row — the 0305 and 0507
grid strings are the same gradient to within the noise floor, at the same
price (0.020 / 0.017). 0.7–0.9 is a *different* pull: 1.8 × the coherent
signal per draw, but at cos 0.27 against a 0.95 floor and nearly orthogonal
to what the chain bought. The § 2 "level with `scene_piece` at 0.7–0.9" is
therefore not "the same thing cheaper" — it is another thing, unread on any
ruler, and the one trained read of pieces at 0.7–0.9 (scene, `micro_cf_0922`:
1 / 32 vs 7 / 32) went the wrong way. **v2 keeps `grid_string` in the law's
piece band, 0.5–0.7 (0305: 0.3–0.5), and `windows.py` gains no grid-piece
row.** The 0.7–0.9 grid-string pull is a `run0925_72` arm if ever, on
`native_sent`, never a band change on the gradient alone.
