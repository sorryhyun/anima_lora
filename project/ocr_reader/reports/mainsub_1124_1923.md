## Interpretation

- Against the first v7 every gate is level or higher, none past p 0.05 on its own; the direction
  is the same on all of them. Two things changed together, the labels and the micro-batch (8 × 2
  against 4 × 4, the same 16 crops a step), so the run does not say which one moved the gates.
- The Manga109 test SFX gap to v6 (85.2 % against 81.9 %) stays.
- The pod's speed came from the batch, not the card: at 4 × 4 the RTX PRO 6000 ran barely faster
  than the 5070 Ti with the GPU at about 70 %, so the job is bound by per-step overhead; 8 crops a
  micro-step doubled the rate. The memory note for the next pod run (bs 16, no accumulation) builds
  on this.
