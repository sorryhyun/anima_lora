## Interpretation

- At full length the one-prompt reader is at `sfxpc`'s level on speech and above it on the
  doujin SFX strict count; the strict gain shrinks to inside the noise heart-blind, so much of it
  is heart marks. The 1k run's collapse on SFX (58 / 593) was length, not the main / sub form.
- Manga109 test SFX is under `sfxpc` by 1.9 points, and the paired count says it is not noise.
  Under main / sub an SFX crop that also shows speech has the speech as main, so some of these
  rows may be scored against a label the policy no longer asks for; the misses were not read.
- Against v6 the picture is the same as against `sfxpc`, larger on Manga109 SFX (−4.4 points):
  the cost of reading detector boxes by their largest text shows on a gate that scores one
  polygon per sound effect.
- KO and ZH are above both references with the kana leak near zero; n is 48 each and the gap was
  not tested.
- The empty-answer share fell from 99.0 % to 96.2 % with length. Whether the 11 are real subs
  the labels lack or re-reads of the main needs the crops.
- Second epoch: main +2.3 points, sub +1.3, runaways 75 -> 51; still rising at the end.
