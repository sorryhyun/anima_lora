## Interpretation

- The negatives bring empty answers back on Japanese crops (0 % -> 72.6 %) without a visible
  cost on main or sub, but short of the 98.3 % the all-Manga109 negatives gave. With 96 % of
  the negatives Korean, part of the "no sub" answer is likely tied to the script; 49 of the 80
  misses still re-read the main.
- The 292 crops are mains with no kanji, the rule the user took out on 11-22; some of them may
  hold small text a reviewer would call a sub, so 100 % is not the ceiling. Reading the 80
  crops would settle how many are real misses.
- A larger Japanese share of the negatives is the likely next lever: more reviewed JA rows with
  an empty sub, or kana-only mains back as negatives if the user accepts them.
