# `mainsub_1127_0706` – `0709`: tower / LM parametrization, LoRA against DoRA, 1k steps (2026-11-27)

## Facts

### Design

- Written after the submit, before any result was read. The user (2026-11-27), after the tower /
  LM split and DoRA were discussed: "머 해보자 다만 tower + lm 둘 다 lora인 경우, 둘 다 dora인
  경우도 같이 비겨해보자".
- Question: does the LM need full FT, and does DoRA on the LoRA targets read better than LoRA, at
  1,000 steps? The 1k read picks which arms get a 2-epoch run; it does not ship one.
- Four arms, one seed each, all on the labels and `TARGET_NORM = 12` as of 2026-11-27 07:06, seed
  0, stock base. Shared: `finetune_vl16_lora.py --mainsub --steps 1000 --val_limit 500
  --tower_rank 64 --ckpt_lm_only --compile` (bs 4 × 4, lr 1e-4 on every LoRA, token-mean loss).

  | run | tower + projector | LM decoder layers |
  |---|---|---|
  | `mainsub_1127_0706` (control) | LoRA r64 / α 128 | full FT, lr 1e-5 (`--lm_full`) |
  | `mainsub_1127_0707` | DoRA r64 / α 128 (`--dora`) | full FT, lr 1e-5 |
  | `mainsub_1127_0708` | LoRA r64 / α 128 | LoRA r64 / α 128 (`--rank 64`) |
  | `mainsub_1127_0709` | DoRA r64 / α 128 | DoRA r64 / α 128 (`--rank 64 --dora`) |

  `0706` is the v7 recipe at 1k (`mainsub_1124_1745`'s command) on today's labels.
- What the arms cannot separate: `0709` changes the LM side and the adapter type together; its
  read against `0706` goes through `0707` and `0708`.
- `--dora` is new (peft `use_dora`, `ocr/finetune_vl16_lora.py`); DoRA under `--compile` had not
  run before this submit. The first `0707` job (`20261127-070621-62ce43`) failed at the first
  step: peft's `dequantize_module_weight` calls `str.startswith`, a graph break in every compiled
  layer, and the token axis then failed its dynamic guard (`ConstraintViolationError`). Under
  `--compile --dora` the trainer now binds it to the layer's weight (the base Linears are plain
  bf16); `torch._dynamo.explain` on a DoRA Linear: 4 breaks before, 0 after.
- The first `0709` job (`20261127-070621-deb419`, patched) passed compile and ran out of memory
  in the first tower forward, in the projector's DoRA weight norm (14.5 GB allocated of 15.5).
  Resubmitted with `--grad_ckpt` (tower checkpointed too), `0709` (`20261127-073409-c31ec0`) ran
  at 6.2 crops/s, 3.7 GB, and was cancelled at step 600 on the user's word ("bsz 2*8로 ㄱㄱ").
- At `--bs 2 --grad_accum 8 --ckpt_lm_only` both DoRA arms ran out of memory at the same place
  (`20261127-080702-c67e39`, `-5779fb`): the token budget stayed 3,072, so a two-crop batch could
  still pad to 3,072 tower tokens. Probes `smoke_dora_compile_1127` / `smoke_dora_eager_1127`

  3,072 with LoRA: 12.6 GB).
- Both DoRA arms run at `--bs 2 --grad_accum 8 --token_budget 2048 --ckpt_lm_only` (a crop caps

### DoRA arms cancelled

- `0709` at bs 2 × 8, budget 2,048 (`20261127-080941-4d29c3`) ran at 7.3 crops/s, 12.7 GB, and
  was cancelled at about step 300 with `0707` still queued. The user (2026-11-27): "그렇다고
  dora를 또 돌릴순 없을거같아서 그냥 dora는 취소하자".
- Cause of the speed and memory, from the peft 0.20 source: the LoRA layer casts `x` to the
  adapter dtype (fp32) before the variant, and with `lora_dropout` > 0 the DoRA variant does not
  reuse the bf16 base output but recomputes `F.linear(dropout(x), W.to(fp32))`; the trainer sets
  no TF32. Each DoRA Linear so runs one extra full-size fp32 matmul and keeps an fp32 copy of `W`
  and fp32 activations for backward. Not measured layer by layer.
- `smoke_dora_peak_1127` (`--largest_first`, 3 steps, bs 2, budget 2,048): peak 13.1 GB at 2,188
  tower tokens.

## Interpretation

- The two arms split by gate: the LM LoRA reads the in-domain Manga109 SFX slightly more often
  and the out-of-domain doujin SFX and speech less often, K3 level. The doujin gate was named
  beforehand as unstable at 1k across runs; within this pair both doujin counts lean one way.
- P2b (`_archive/ocr_reader/history/reports/p2b_tower_lora_lm_full.md`) showed the same split
  from the other side: moving to LM full FT raised the doujin gate and left COO level. The two
  records together point at the LM full FT for the doujin reads, with one seed on each side.
- On the line's gate (doujin SFX), the 1k read gives no reason to move off LM full FT; the LM
  LoRA's 3.4 GB saving is the one thing it buys.
