## Facts

### Design

- Written after the submit, before any result was read. The user (2026-11-24), on a 1k A/B of the
  per-crop mean: "ㅇㅋ 현재 레이블 기준으로 해보자".
- Question: does a per-crop mean of the loss raise the SFX reads at 1,000 steps? No rise -> the
  arm is dropped. A rise -> a 2-epoch run decides whether the gap to v6 on Manga109 test SFX
  (80.8 % against 85.2 %, [`mainsub_1124_0151`](mainsub_1124_0151.md)) closes; the 1k read does
  not answer that.
- `mainsub_1124_1502` (control): the command of [`mainsub_1124_0138`](mainsub_1124_0138.md) on
  today's labels, `finetune_vl16_lora.py --mainsub --steps 1000 --val_limit 500 --tower_rank 64
  --lm_full --ckpt_lm_only --compile --loss_mean token`, stock base, seed 0.
- `mainsub_1124_1503`: `--loss_mean token` -> `crop`, nothing else changed. The CE is averaged
  inside each crop and then over the batch's crops (`train_loss(crop_mean=True)`); the token mean
  averages over the batch's target tokens.
- What the arm cannot separate: every short target gains weight together, the SFX mains and the
  empty answers (`</s>` alone) alike.
- The logged `train_loss` is each arm's own mean and is not compared across the arms.
- Read: Manga109 test SFX (2,558) and speech (2,559) under `OCR:` (`eval_manga109.py --manifest
  sfxp_gt`), paired between the arms; the in-training val main 500 / sub 500; the doujin gate
  (`eval_sfx.py --pad cap4`) as a number, with its empty answers.
- Bars: spread unknown (no seed twin). On the doujin gate 1k runs have read strict 58, 110 and
  173 (`mainsub_1121_1806`, `sfxpc_1118_2037`, `sfxpc_1118_0031`), so it is not the read here.
- Cost: two runs of about 13 min, with about 3 min of evals each.

## Interpretation

- The SFX rows' share of the loss rose about 2.7 times and Manga109 test SFX moved by one point,
  inside what a paired count of 107 / 133 can tell from chance. By the question's own rule the
  arm did not show a rise: no 2-epoch run is proposed on it.
- The one-point gain sits in the 1–2 character labels. Speech and both val reads did not move, so
  the lighter long targets cost nothing visible at this length.
- The doujin gate leans the other way heart-blind while more reads hold a heart. It was named
  beforehand as too unstable at 1k to read, and the two p values do not change that.
- A larger weight on the SFX crops did not make them train faster. Both arms see the same crops
  in the same order (about 2,170 SFX crops in 16,000), and the 2-epoch run's gain over 1k came
  with length, so the SFX read at this stage looks bounded by how many crops have been seen
  rather than by their share of each step. That would favour oversampling or a longer run over a
  reweighted loss; neither was run here.
