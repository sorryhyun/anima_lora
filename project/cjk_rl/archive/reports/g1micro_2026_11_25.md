## Interpretation

- On scenes and seeds no update saw, the trained rows read their glyph on 132 pages against
  59, with 13 of 16 glyphs up. The read noise known so far (a few pages a glyph) is far under
  that. The header's first answer applies (the control, then the longer run); on the user's
  read the line goes on to the longer run without the control.
- The gain is the asked glyph and not lettering in general: the next glyph's solo stays at 0,
  extra letters fall on 13 glyphs, and the cross-entropy of the asked glyph falls on 14.
- Rows that started at zero can be started: 人 0 → 7, and 太 and 味 from 2 to 12 and 10. 行 and
  楽 did not start in 8 steps, and 行's cross-entropy rose.
- を went the other way (2 → 0, two more blank pages). One glyph of 16 going down is not evidence by
  itself; the sheet shows the trained row lettering a larger glyph the
  reader does not take as を, which is worth a look at full size before more steps go into it.
- ぬ and 死 hold their solo while their pages change (ぬ: four pages lose their box; 死: more
  extra letters). Rows that already read well gain least and may be the first to be pushed
  off; a longer run should watch them.
- G0d's low cosines did not stop learning. A row's successive 16-render means agree at 0.07,
  and eight such steps with momentum were enough. The per-render direction is noisy, but the
  shared part is there and accumulates.
- Eight steps moved a row 0.15 – 0.18 norms; none of the sixteen is saturated (best 15 of 16),
  so the steps to a row's ceiling are not known yet.
