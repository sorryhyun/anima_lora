### Interpretation

- From jp_v1's rows, 16 guarded steps put the kana where 4 steps from the shared start put
  them, and 69 pages under 6 steps, with the rows split about evenly. The shared start is
  worth ten or so steps a row here, 2.7 times the training.
- For some rows the shared start is more than a saving. え, っ, の, グ and ヂ letter Latin or
  another glyph at jp_v1's start and do not leave it in 16 steps of their own gradient; from
  the shared start the same rows read 10 – 14 of 16 at 6 steps.
- The rows g2b reads better are partly the ones g2a had pushed past their glyph (タ, く, い,
  ぎ, ブ). Whether that is the guards or the start cannot be told from this run.
- The taper did little before step 12: the rows were under half read until then. The
  returns came mostly from rows under half read and on falls of 4 – 6, which a step's own
  spread produces; the rise after a return is what a window picked for being low does with
  no return as well.
- Doubling is unchanged in count, and ヒ doubled under the guards. The loss on one picked
  crop does not see the second glyph, and none of the three keys changes that.
- The 164 rows' common displacement points the way g1long's 16 rows' did, at half the
  length. The shared direction is a property of the frame and the reader more than of the
  rows it was measured on.
- For the kanji this points to the shared start with the guarded step: the start gives the
  rows that cannot start alone their way in, and the taper and a stricter return are for
  the rows that then read early.
