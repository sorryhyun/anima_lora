## Interpretation

- The two terms are of one size on the row, so the user's 0.2 can be taken as a plain loss
  weight: it puts the hayai term at about a fifth of the tuned term on a typical render. The
  config header's first answer applies.
- The terms share a direction on a render (0.21 against a null at 0), which two renders of one
  reader do not. Most of what a render says to the row is said by both readers; the pixel
  cosine is small because each reader's gradient sits on its own patch grid.
- About a third of the renders have the two terms opposed on the row (cosine under 0). At 0.2
  the hayai term cannot turn those steps; it shortens them. Whether that is the readers
  disagreeing about the glyph or the last step's row gradient being close to one direction
  with a noisy sign is not separated here.
- Where the aux term would matter most is the render the tuned reader already accepts and hayai
  does not (the malformed 死 of `reader_pair_2026_11_25.md`). This run holds one such render,
  so its size there is not measured.
- Cost is not a reason either way: 0.05 s a render on a 0.73 s render.
