  LLM adapter once per arm and caption). The path it replaces prepared each caption inside the
  loop: 0.16 s a render at two renders a caption (job `20261125-093350-0b3107`). One caption

  2.4 s a forward, 0.30 s an image), the pixel head 0.010 s.
- Reads: 8.8 pages a second, 8 pages a call with one detector forward a page. With the detector
  batched and 16 pages a call (job `20261125-101112-e40016`, 256 of these renders): 10.7 pages
  and 21.4 crops a second; 242 of 256 pages come back with the same boxes and reads, the other
  14 with the same boxes and another read on one crop.
- Later renders go 4 images a forward (user, 11-25).

## Interpretation

- The premise's level is low on this stack to begin with: a one-token EN word is lettered alone
  on 14 % of plain pages and 27 % of bubble pages, and almost all of that on three of eight
  scenes. The rows sit under it in both frames, hiragana at a third of the EN level with a
  bubble, kanji near zero.
- The plain frame is where § 2's argument holds (a scene letters almost nothing by itself), and
  it is also where the rows give G-R nothing to rank: half the pages hold no text and seven
  JA groups in eight are constant. That is G0b's stop condition for G-R on the plain frame as
  proposed.
- With `speech bubble` the reward moves inside half the groups, but the page is no longer the
  quote's: the same scenes letter 16 letters a page with no clause, so `g_p` there mostly
  measures the scene. The clause without a bubble also adds text that is not the string (1.3
  letters a page without it, 4 – 7 with it).
- The scene decides more than the frame. A pool of scenes that letter nothing with no clause in
  either frame, and on which the EN word lands, would keep § 2's argument and give the bubble
  frame's spread; whether such scenes are common enough is not known from eight.
- G1's sixteen rows cannot be picked "across the range of solo" from this run: a string's count
  is its scene's. A crossed grid on a few clean scenes would give that.
- The pixel head can stand in for the VAE as the reward's eyes at this size.
