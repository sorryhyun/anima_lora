# G0d — the gradient G-D's path puts on a row (2026-11-25)

Covers the build of G-D's path (`../src/gd.py`) and its first measurement, run
`g0d_1125_1119` (`../configs/g0d_1125_1119.toml`, `../probes/gd_g0d.py`, daemon job
`20261125-111910-5d3f29`, 9.9 min). Result:
`../results/20261125-1129-gd_g0d-g0d_1125_1119/` (local: `result.json`, `records.json` a
render, `grads.pt` the row gradients); pages `output/cjk_rl/gd_g0d/g0d_1125_1119/` (local).

## Facts

### The path

- `gd.Policy`: a render is sampled with no graph and every step's input kept; a step is then
  run again with a graph and the cotangent of its output pulled to its input and to the text
  embedding (`Policy.vjp`). The last step's output is the pixel head's image, an earlier
  step's the next x_t. The embedding's cotangent is per render; `Policy.row_grads` pulls it
  through the LLM adapter to `raw`.
- `gd.Reader.ce`: ids, mask and labels from the reader's processor on the crop as an image
  (prompt + string + EOS, loss on the string and the EOS, the training collate of
  `project/ocr_reader/ocr/finetune_vl16_lora.py`); `pixel_values` from the page tensor
  (crop, bicubic resize to the processor's size, normalise, patchify).
- `gd.Reader.det_scores`: AnimeText's letterbox on the tensor (an area resize written as two
  matrices), the model's per-anchor score kept in the graph.
- The head's image is clamped to [-1, 1] for the reads and the gradient passes the clamp as
  identity. In a crop a median of 11 – 17 % of the values lie past the clamp and carry
  10 – 14 % of the ce gradient's absolute mass.
- Checks, in the run's `check`:

  | check | number |
  |---|---|
  | `Policy.rollout` against `generate_body` + `pixel_decode`, one group | max abs pixel difference 0 |
  | row gradient on rows other than the glyph's | 0 |
  | tensor `pixel_values` against the processor's (normalised units; 16 renders) | max 0.103, mean 0.004 |
  | ce on the tensor's `pixel_values` against ce on the processor's | max abs difference 0.031 |
  | detector top score, tensor letterbox against `prepare` on the same uint8 page | max abs difference 0.013 |

### Memory and time

768², the reader and the detector resident, a group = 4 renders.

- A backward forward holds 1 render: 4 and 2 a forward ran out of memory (smoke job
  `20261125-111605-c262df`). Sampling stays 4 a forward.
- Peak allocated: 8.5 GiB in the image-gradient phase (reader and detector backward),
  12.0 GiB in a step's backward. The four-step backward peaks where the one-step one does.
- Median seconds a group by phase:

  | phase | s |
  |---|---|
  | sampling, 4 steps, no graph (with the embedding) | 1.62 |
  | read (detector + reader) | 0.18 |
  | reader ce forward + backward, one label | 0.135 |
  | detector score forward + backward, 4 pages | 0.19 |
  | one step's backward, 4 renders | 0.94 – 0.96 |
  | rows from the embedding's cotangent, 4 steps × 4 renders | 0.23 |

  Summed for one term set: 3.0 – 3.2 s a group with the last step's backward, 5.9 – 6.1 s
  with all four. Not timed as a training loop.

## Interpretation

- Size is not what stops G-D. The last step's ce gradient on a render that misses its glyph
  is five decades over ε, five more than P1's, and the card holds it at one render a
  backward.
- **A render's row gradient is large but points its own way: two seeds of the same glyph
  agree no more than two different glyphs do (cosine 0.01 against a null of 0.003 – 0.017).**
  What a row shares over renders is small.
- Most of a render's ce gradient does not depend on the label: on a missed crop the gradient
  toward the asked glyph and toward another glyph have a median cosine near 0.77. A likely
  reading is that both mostly push the crop away from what the reader reads there now.
- A part that does follow the label exists and repeats over renders: the difference between
  the two labels' gradients has cosine 0.025 between two renders of a glyph (last step)
  against 0.001 between glyphs. At that cosine the mean of the difference over n renders
  reaches cosine 0.5 to its shared direction near n = 13 and 0.7 near n = 38; the whole ce
  gradient, at 0.009 between two `miss` renders, needs about 37 for 0.5. So an update that
  moves a row should average tens of that row's renders, by accumulation or by a long Adam β1.
- On this measure the last step is not worse than all four: the label part repeats at 0.025
  on the last step and 0.015 with all four, at half the seconds. The four-step gradient is
  larger (the first two steps carry most of it) but no more consistent. This does not test
  the plan's concern that the last step cannot make a glyph appear on a blank page: only 10
  pages had no box.
- The norm of a render's gradient spans three decades (0.23 to 677 at the 10th percentile and
  the maximum). A mean over renders is then one or two renders' gradient unless each is
  clipped or normalised first.
- The detector term's cosine to ce is within the null's range on pages that hold the glyph
  (0.04 on the last step), so the header's rule passes it without the measurement saying it
  helps. Its own consistency over seeds is not measured (2 pairs).
- The page term at weight 1 does nothing on the last step. A weight in the thousands would be
  needed to level it with ce, and its cosine to ce is near 0, so it neither helps nor fights
  the glyph on these pages.
- Two reads of the same picture disagree on the case for 26 of 128 pages (in-memory against
  WebP, 4 against 16 pages a call). The 0.6 line and the box / no-box line move with the
  read, which is part of the per-render noise above.
