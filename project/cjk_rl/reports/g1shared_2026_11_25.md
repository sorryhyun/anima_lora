## Interpretation

- The shared start is worth steps, and fewer than eight. Four steps from it read over the
  parent's eight; eight steps from it read between the parent's eight and sixteen under the
  tuned reader and at the parent's sixteen under hayai. On the training side the second pass
  from the shared start sits where the parent's third does. Taken together it saves about
  four to six of the sixteen steps.
- It starts a row the parent could not. 行 never read in 24 steps from the start rows; here
  it reads on 6 pages (hayai 9) after 8. In the parent its displacement was the one with no
  part along what the other rows share (`row_line_2026_11_25.md`).
- あ is the cost. It read 12 of 16 before a single step of its own, 13 of 16 on the training
  side at step 6, and two more steps of the same length put a second kana in the bubble. This
  is ぬ's late fall in the parent, sooner and harder, because a row from the shared start
  reaches its glyph while the step is still full length. A shared start needs the step to
  shorten or the row to stop once it reads; a fixed count of steps a row does not fit it.
- The run does not separate one thing: the other rows' displacement was trained on these
  training scenes and partly these seeds. The read pages were in no update.
