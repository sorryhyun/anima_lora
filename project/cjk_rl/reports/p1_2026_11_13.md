## What the run could and could not test

- A row was in a median of 2 groups (proposal § 1), and each group's advantage comes from 8
  renders whose spread is in large part reader error (`p0_2026_11_13.md`, P0b).
- The step was small on top of that. `grpo.py` averages log π over latent dimensions, which
  leaves the row gradient near Adam's ε, and the largest row moved 0.05 of its norm.
- So the run does not separate "the GRPO gradient on rows is noise" from "the rows barely
  moved". Both readings lead to the same next step: the estimator fixes (proposal § 3.6) and
  parameters that every group updates.
