## Interpretation

- The header's second branch: the read of strings the run did not train on rose over the
  start rows on both sets and in every length bin, and ended under `sentw`'s 400 groups in
  each of them. The whole strings and the kana alone are the two rows where it is level
  with or over `sentw`. With no twin the gaps have no size to be read against; the
  per-string mean (0.048 ± 0.016) says the rise over the start rows is not one string's.
- What ended the run is the row budget, not the strings. The rows most strings hold were
  at 4 steps within the first 600 groups and took nothing after; the last 1 232 groups
  stepped only rows still short of 4 (88 steps) and moved the val read by 0.006. More
  strings on this budget would train the rows still under 4 steps and nothing else.
- `sentw` gave its frequent rows 10 steps or more (`ー` drifted 0.32 row norms) and read
  higher on strings; this run held every row to 0.12 and the kana alone did not fall
  (351 → 361, where `sentw` lost 6). The two go together as the budget's trade, but the
  six keys changed together: one seed a string and `skip_hit` are in the same difference.
- Inside the 4 steps a row's training score does not move (5.2 → 5.4 of 16) and the
  successive gradients agree a little less than `sentw`'s. That fits steps too few to
  show on the row's own pages; it does not say a longer walk would keep its direction.
- The header's third branch is met on the count at 5 – 8 glyphs (0.94 → 1.07) and not on
  its content: the string's own glyphs are repeated less than at the start, and the extra
  glyphs are ones the string does not hold. The arm with the neighbour's-window positions
  left out answers to repeats, so the count alone is a thin reason for it; the user's
  sheets decide.
- What would be proposed next, the user's call: the same recipe again from these rows
  (4 more steps a row, about this run's 2.2 h of training and its reads), or the budget
  raised in one run. Either tests the budget as the limit; `[start]` would have to take
  the marks' rows along with the kana. The user's own renders on the pack come first.
