# Before the sentence loop: kana strings on kmix's rows, a text swap, the quote's characters, the labels (2026-11-26)

The two forward probes and the counts the sentence stage was planned on, before `src/s1.py`
ran. Moved here from the sentence plan (`../archive/sent_rl.md`, "Facts it rests on") when
the line's documents were folded together; no number is new.

## 1. Kana strings on kmix's rows (`wread_1126_1207`)

### Facts

- Forward only, 48 pool strings (12 a length at 2, 3, 4 and 5 – 8 glyphs), 96 pages a
  length, jp_v1's rows → kmix's rows (`kmix_1126_1158`).
- The whole string alone in a box: 19 → 52 at 2 glyphs, 2 → 21 at 3, 2 → 13 at 4, 0 → 0
  at 5 – 8.
- Share of positions hit: 0.41 → 0.76, 0.28 → 0.62, 0.27 → 0.53, 0.20 → 0.35.
- kmix's hit share by position: 0.66, 0.52, 0.47, 0.38, 0.26.
- Glyphs drawn over glyphs asked: 0.84 at 5 – 8. The first glyph alone on 11 of 384 pages.
- Spread unknown. `results/20261126-1215-row_line-wread_1126_1207/` (local).

### Interpretation

- Rows trained one glyph a caption already carry into short strings, so a string run starts
  from a working state. What it has to mend is glyphs dropped from a string and the fall
  along its length.

## 2. A render's text swapped at a step (`tswap_1126_1209`)

### Facts

- Forward only, kmix's rows: a render's first k steps on string A, the rest on string B.
  k = 0 is B's own render, k = 2 gives B to σ 0.75 → 0.5 → 0, k = 3 to σ 0.5 → 0 alone.
  Small kana folded; 128 pages a cell for a glyph, 96 for a length.
- Spread unknown. `results/20261126-1227-text_swap-tswap_1126_1209/` (local).

| | B alone, k 0 / 2 / 3 | A alone, k 0 / 2 / 3 | B's positions hit, k 0 / 2 / 3 | A's positions hit, k 0 / 2 / 3 |

| 5 – 8 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0.36 / 0.35 / 0.22 | 0.12 / 0.11 / 0.18 |

- The user's call on this table (11-26): the sentence run's backward covers the last two
  steps, σ 0.75 → 0.5 → 0.

### Interpretation

- Most of what a page letters is set in the step σ 0.75 → 0.5: with B's text on the last two
  steps the page is close to B's own render, and with it on the last step alone the page
  mostly keeps A, for a single glyph as for a string.
- The last step still moves the read part of the way, and its gradient alone took the single
  glyphs from 0.28 to 0.68 of their pages, since a row moved by it acts at every step. So the
  swap does not say the one-step backward fails on strings; it says the two-step backward
  reaches the step where the string is set.

## 3. The quote's characters and the labels

### Facts

- Where a quote's characters go on the T5 side (the active pack with `glyph_route`; jp_v1's
  delta in row norms): a kana its own row (1.2 – 1.5); `ー` its own row (1.52; `―` folds to
  it); `「` `」` 0.9; `。` 0.48, `、` 0.35; `〜` and `~` one row, 0.42; `♥` 0.46, `♡` 0.48, a
  row each; a dot run inside a string one row, 0.38; `！` `？` fold to ASCII and are T5's own
  tokens, with no row to train.
- The OCR reader's main labels (`load_mainsub`, `OCR:` rows), distinct targets made of kana
  and those marks: train 28 975 (2 – 4 glyphs 9 552, 5 – 8 11 739, 9 – 12 4 570, 13 – 20
  2 565, over 20 460), val 3 517, of them 2 396 in no train label. 12 547 of the train
  targets hold a line break; `…` is in 5 738, `ー` in 5 628, `!` in 4 913, `?` in 2 827, `♡`
  in 244.
- A run of two or more `・`: in 2 343 of 115 529 train mains and 294 of 11 634 val mains;
  in 1 494 of `sent_strings_s1126`'s 24 891 train strings and 131 of its 1 844 val strings.
- The P1 pool (`output/cjk_rl/pool/pool.json`, local) holds 355 kana-only strings.

### Interpretation

- Reusing the reader's labels as prompts does not touch the reader. It can flatter the read:
  a reader writes a sentence it was trained on more readily from poor glyphs. The val-only
  strings, hayai beside the tuned reader and the user's sheets are the checks.
- A row is one vector at every position, so order and count over a long string may be out of
  its reach; the read by length shows where that begins.
