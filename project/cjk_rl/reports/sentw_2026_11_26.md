# The window loss — a row's gradient on a string, from the micro arm's log to a micro arm that moves the read (2026-11-26)

Five reads in the order they were made: `sentm_1126_1331`'s log and pages read again (no
GPU), two gradient probes (`sgrad_1126_1436`, `sgrad_1126_1511`), the attention probe
between them (`aloc_1126_1501`), and the micro arm on the loss they led to
(`sentw_1126_1528`). Each run's settings, question and gate are in its config header. The
last two parts hold what a longer run would spend and the split of the backward by step,
both asked by the user while the micro arm ran.

# sentm's log and pages, read again

Opened by the user's question (11-26): "문자열 학습이 뭔가 잘 안됐는데, 이거 레이블에서의 row
등장횟수 그래디언트 보정이 들어가야 하려나? 아니면 ... fold?", and on the second half: "fold

## Facts

- `s1.py`'s step is a fixed length along the row's momentum (`eta` row norms, times the
  taper), so a row's gradient scaled by a count leaves the step where it was; what a row's
  frequency sets is how many steps it takes.
- `sentm_1126_1331`'s log, the cosine of a row's successive mean gradients, returns left
  out, ± the standard error over pairs: 0.008 ± 0.011 (264 pairs); rows of 10 steps or
  more 0.013 (139), of 3 – 9 steps −0.002 (124); by the step's share of positions hit
  (under 0.25, 0.25 – 0.5, over 0.5) 0.006, −0.008, 0.021. `g2b_1126_0058`: 0.083 ± 0.004
  (2 240).
- The start rows' string pages (val and seen, 384 pages, 2 456 positions), positions hit
  and whole strings under each fold of the count: as trained 0.357 and 21; a small kana as
  its full-size glyph 0.368 and 23; a kana without its voiced mark, へ as ヘ 0.374 and 22;
  hiragana as katakana 0.364 and 21; the three together 0.393 and 24. By the asked glyph:
  full-size kana 0.309 of 1 800, small kana 0.438 of 208, marks 0.511 of 448.
- `!` and `?`: the draw's 24 891 train strings hold 5 991 and 2 377 of the ASCII forms and
  none of the full-width ones; both readers wrote ASCII on every read page; the pack
  folds `！` to `!` on the T5 side. They are T5's own tokens, in the loss's target, with no
  row to train.

## Interpretation

- The rows that stepped most have no more direction than the rest, so a correction by
  frequency would have guarded the frequent rows against drift and taught nothing.
- The folds return 87 of 1 579 missed positions. The small rows kmix trained on full-size
  targets read above the full-size kana in a string. Normalisation is not where the read
  is lost.
- What `sentm` changed from the glyph stage is the caption and the backward, and the log
  cannot tell them apart; a string's cross-entropy also sends every position's term to
  every row of the quote. Both go to the probe below.

# `sgrad_1126_1511` — the gradient of a glyph read on its own window

## Facts

- `sgrad_1126_1436`'s rows, strings, scenes and seeds, 12.3 min; `win`: the two readers'
  cross-entropy of the row's glyph alone (its tokens, no EOS) on its attention windows;
  `wall`: the same summed over every glyph of the string that has a row.
  `results/20261126-1527-sent_grad-sgrad_1126_1511/` (local).

  | caption | loss | across, last step | across, the step before | across, their sum | norm of the sum, median |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | the glyph alone | whole | 0.032 ± 0.009 | 0.015 ± 0.007 | 0.024 ± 0.010 | 0.051 |
  | a string | the string's cross-entropy | 0.003 ± 0.004 | 0.003 ± 0.004 | 0.005 ± 0.004 | 0.193 |
  | a string | the row's glyph on its windows (`win`) | 0.014 ± 0.010 | 0.007 ± 0.005 | 0.016 ± 0.008 | 0.244 |
  | a string | every glyph on its window, summed (`wall`) | −0.002 ± 0.003 | −0.003 ± 0.002 | −0.001 ± 0.003 | 0.938 |

- The first two rows repeat `sgrad_1126_1436`'s to the third decimal.
- `win`, the sum: over 0 on 5 of 8 rows; without `あ` 0.012 ± 0.008; against the glyph's
  last-step part of another group 0.004 ± 0.003. A first run of the probe ran out of
  memory holding every window's reader graph beside the last step's; a window's graph is
  now freed before the next.
- The first run of this probe, and of the two-loss form of `sgrad_1126_1436`, found that
  a compiled reader or block takes one backward a graph (`retain_graph` is refused); a
  second loss re-runs the readers and the last step.

## Interpretation

- The window loss gives a row about two thirds of the agreement its glyph alone gives
  through two steps, at 2 standard errors from 0. The probe does not settle it.
- The sum over every glyph's window gives a row nothing: a row acts on the pixels of the
  other glyphs' windows through the DiT, at five times the size of its own. One backward a
  render for every row is out, and the loop backs a row at a time.

# What a longer run would spend

Asked by the user while the micro arm ran: "만약 이 레시피대로 학습한다 치면, 길이 분포나
단어/문장 다양성은 어떻게 하는게 좋아?" Nothing here is decided or run.

## Facts

- Cost, from the micro arm's smoke: 0.85 s a render (the forwards and the read) and 0.58 s
  a row-render (a row's two-step backward and its windows' loss).
- The draw's 24 891 train strings: 2 – 4 / 5 – 8 / 9 – 12 glyphs 0.414 / 0.418 / 0.168,
  5.71 glyphs and 4.56 trained rows a string. The ten most held rows (`…` 6 391, `っ`
  5 457, `ー` 4 849, `い` 3 844, `ん` 3 656 …) are 0.33 of the row-strings; 82 of the 172
  rows are in fewer than 256 strings, 15 in fewer than 64 (ヲ ヵ ヶ ヂ ヅ in fewer than 16).
- A render a string, with a backward only for the rows still short of a budget: 64
  renders a row take 5 588 strings and 10 429 row-renders and 157 rows reach it; 256 take
  15 220 strings and 33 735 row-renders and 90 rows reach it.
- `sgrad_1126_1511`, the window loss through two steps: renders of one string agree at
  0.046, of different strings at 0.016; renders whose position the page's read missed
  0.034 ± 0.022, hit 0.008 ± 0.008; strings of 6 glyphs or fewer 0.025 (268 pairs), of 7
  or more 0.011 (588), pooled.

## Interpretation

- The unit of cost is a row-render, so a row's frequency is set by which rows a render
  backs; `max_steps` already leaves a capped row out, and with a backward a row a capped
  row costs nothing. 64 renders a row is about 3 h, 256 about 9 h.
- A seed a string in place of four puts 16 strings in a row's step at the same cost.
- The labels' own mix of lengths, with a gate a position, reads as the better choice than
  a mix weighted to short strings: a position whose centre is within half a glyph of its
  neighbour's is left out, and a position whose window already reads is not backed.
- The rows the draw cannot fill take glyph groups or stay as kmix has them.
