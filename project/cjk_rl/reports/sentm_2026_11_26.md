## Interpretation

- 400 groups did not move the read of strings, on the val strings or on the train
  labels, and the kana alone are where they were. With no twin none of the table's
  moves can be called a loss; the short strings went down under both counts and are the
  cell to watch.
- A row's mean gradient points somewhere unrelated at each step, where in the glyph
  stage two successive steps agreed a little. The momentum then walks on noise: the rows
  that stepped most drifted a quarter of a row norm without reading better, and `か`
  walked off its glyph.
- The run changed the caption (a glyph → a string) and the backward (one step → two)
  together, so it does not say which of them took the agreement away. That is what
  `sgrad_1126_1422` was for.
- The kana alone held mostly because most rows barely moved; the eight that stepped ten
  times or more read 21 → 19.

## Interpretation

- The labels are lettered vertically five times in six; the renders are wide two times in
  three, and nine in ten at 3 – 4 glyphs.
- A tall page is read about half as well as a wide one. The count cannot tell a model
  that letters a column badly from a reader that reads a rendered column badly. Either
  way the loss as it stands is satisfied more often by a wide page, so nothing in it
  pulls toward a column.
- Without the prompt, the levers are a weight on a render by whether its box stands the
  way the label did, a backward that reaches the first two steps (where a page's layout
  is set; `back_steps = 4`, about 6.2 s a group), and scenes whose bubbles are tall. None
  is tried.
