# lora_blocks reports

One file per run or probe. Conclusions drawn across them: [`../findings/`](../findings/).

- [copy_vs_step.md](copy_vs_step.md) — the copy head forms at steps ~175–225 of any pair set, after the pixels already copy; training past it buys breadth.
- [p0_gate.md](p0_gate.md) — P0: the gate fixes both shipped LoRAs' "where" failures with no training; `diag1` inside AnimeText boxes removes the watermark sanitize v3 keeps, `boost` outside the layout boxes stops swap_text rewriting unasked regions; `hard` invents content.
- [colorize_inboost.md](colorize_inboost.md) — a copy boost inside colorize v5's text boxes makes strokes heavier and leaves every deformed glyph as it was; the gate cannot fix lettering.
- [hangul_s500.md](hangul_s500.md) — step 3: a Hangul block from C on 600 synthetic font-lettered pages (500 steps) letters thinner than ksplit e2 in the synthetic layout and garbles uncaptioned Japanese; the exact concat on e2 doubles / blurs strokes.
- [hangul_v2_s500.md](hangul_v2_s500.md) — step 3, v2 data (balloons cleaned on JA raw / KO edition, the edition's line layout, bold faces, partial captions): the block alone letters both Korean eval pages right in a bold clean gothic and keeps uncaptioned Japanese; e2 is already crisp there.
- [hangul_ko10.md](hangul_ko10.md) — ten held-out Korean pages (`inputs_ko10/`): v2 alone misspells far more than ksplit e2 (short vowel strokes lost, lines duplicated); the exact concat v2 + e2 fails on every page; e2 breaks on long dense narration; byte-split syllables seen once are drawn right, the unseen `끜` comes out `끔`.
- [hangul_v3_ft.md](hangul_v3_ft.md) — v3 data (flat narration boxes, Regular faces, tail syllables) mixed with e2's pairs, 600 steps from e2: removes e2's pseudo-glyphs on one long narration page, adds garbles and vowel errors elsewhere; not a win.
- [p1_c_fill.md](p1_c_fill.md) — P1: C (400 steps, clean illustrations, text-layout holes under `ref_gate cut`) removes the sanitize eval pages' stamps and watermark under `cut` with no sanitize pair; the copy head forms by step 200.
- [p2_sanitize_ft.md](p2_sanitize_ft.md) — P2: 300 steps of sanitize from C remove text on the hard manga pages as v3 does, from kaiming they leave blocks; neither holds the tone (EMA off: ΔL swings 2–4 between saves in both arms); `cut` on a fine-tune invents on large masks.
- [c_fill_v2.md](c_fill_v2.md) — C v2 (tone, EMA, B&W colorize pairs, 800 steps): copies as C and fixes its darkening on one page, but letters the large masks under `cut` and brightens three pages ~1 L; the tone term runs at 2–3× the FM gradient and swings with σ.
- [swap_dense_copy_init.md](swap_dense_copy_init.md) — swap_text on 1,509 dense re-crawled Korean pages, 500 steps from C v2 against kaiming: copy init does no harm (slightly better outside the text, slightly worse lettering); both fix e2's long narration and lose short-dialogue syllables.
- [swap_dense2516_main.md](swap_dense2516_main.md) — the main run, shipped as swap_text v3: the 2,516 dense Korean pages + en400 from C v2, 2 epochs; ko10 ~11 wrong syllables against ksplit e2's ~15 and no dropped line, the short dialogue back; English exact on 11 of 12 renders.
- [c_fill_mod16_bw10.md](c_fill_mod16_bw10.md) — C with `mod_rank` 16 and no tone term: paired loss 5–14 % below C v2, the tone term was C v2's late gradient swing, and the adapter learns the reference-side modulation the edit tasks share; B&W cut to 10 % (bw10) gives the best copy outside the mask and Δb near 0, but the page stays +0.5–1.1 L bright and `cut` still letters the large masks and now lets the original text back.
- [t2i_rows_ablate.md](t2i_rows_ablate.md) — a t2i style LoRA cut at inference: the delta on the image rows alone renders as the full LoRA, on the text rows alone as no LoRA; blocks 26–41 alone keep the style and the content changes with it; cutting the text queries off the image keys costs Base attributes and does not change what the LoRA does.
- [offset_kv_copy.md](offset_kv_copy.md) — the offset + kv copy block: Base's early positional own-cell heads vanish under `ref_pos offset`, so the copy is the late lookup alone (same b39 h19 / b40 h20 circuit plus b41 h6), forms ~10× later and half as broad; its LoRA norm is 10× the aligned C's, so P2's sanitize ft (lr 5e-5, 300 steps) moves it 10 % and keeps its failures (stamp and lettering return, reframes).
