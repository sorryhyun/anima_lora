# probe_cf: counterfactual-input FM against plain FM on the warm rows (2026-10-06)

From the user (10-06, after `probe_geom_2026_10_06.md`): a draw's plain-FM
gradient on a warm row is ~97 % noise — does the CF loss
(`../finished/cjk_renderable_anima/idea.md`: input noised from a sibling B,
caption A, target the straight line to A) carry more signal per draw?
`idea.md` § 2 argued it should: against a model that believes B the CF
residual is `(x0_A − x0_B)/σ`, deterministic. `probe_cf.py`: the rows at
`seed_fixed_1005_stick080` (f0's start), 24 kana + 24 kanji rows × 24
`sent` items of `sent_kanji`'s data (the glyph once in the line), each item
re-lettered on its own scene / columns / fill / px as a pair A′ / B through
`render_into_scene(ref_text=…)` (the two differ only inside the union box). B
alternates `swap` (another glyph of the target's script) and `dup` (the
neighbour glyph repeated — `プレゼント` → `プレレント`). Per pair one (σ, ε),
σ ~ U(0.5, 0.7) (the upper half of f0's `sent` bands); plain and CF under the
same σ, ε and caption, batch 1, the trainer's box-share loss, the target
row's gradient read. No step. 1 152 pairs, 5.3 min on the daemon →
`output/cjk_anima_reseed/probe_cf/cf57/` (`grads.pt`, `read.json`,
`sheet.png`).

- **CF does not raise the per-draw signal share.** Spearman–Brown ρ per
  draw (median over rows, 24 draws each): kana plain 0.021 / CF 0.017,
  kanji plain 0.031 / CF 0.035. Plain reproduces `probe_geom`'s 0.022–0.024
  on a different draw (item-per-draw, fixed σ band).
- **The correction CF adds is pure noise.** CF − plain on the same item, σ,
  ε: 0.4–0.6 of the plain gradient's norm per draw, pairwise cos across a
  row's draws **0.000 ± 0.004** — within `swap` and within `dup` alike. The
  residual is deterministic per pair, but its projection through the row's
  Jacobian points a different way on every line: the direction "A, not B"
  is item-specific, and nothing common to the row survives 24 draws.
- **CF's gradient is mostly plain's**: per draw cos(CF, plain) 0.77 / 0.82,
  their means 0.81 / 0.90.
- **The caption has leverage on the warm rows**: λ (1 = the caption wins over
  the input) 0.32–0.40 at σ 0.5–0.6, 0.42–0.46 at 0.6–0.7, `dup` below
  `swap`. Gate 0's sensitivity holds; it is not the limit.

## Read

The CF residual has no ε-variance, but what scatters a row's gradient is
`∂v/∂e_A` across items, not the target. A
target-side lever — CF, ΔFM, any control variate on the residual — leaves
that factor as it is. It also bears on `probe_geom`'s Open (where the noise comes
from): for the CF part at least, the scatter is across items, with σ and ε
fixed by the pairing. Plain FM's own split between ε and item is not
read here.

Not tested: CF trained (Gate 1: whether the correction, summed over
thousands of draws, still moves the row somewhere useful); B drawn as one
fixed confusable per row (a look-alike) instead of random.
