# probe_jl: the rows' Jacobian lens, probe 0 (2026-10-07)

`idea2.md`'s probe 0. M = E[JᵀJ], J = ∂ v_θ(x_σ, c_JA; rows) / ∂ row,
from Gaussian output probes (g = Jᵀv, E_v[g gᵀ] = JᵀJ); the probe on the
item's text box dilated by 2 latent cells gives M_in, on the rest M_out. One
M per band × family (kana / kanji), two fits on disjoint items (A / B).

`probe_jl.py fit`: rows at `seed_fixed_1005_stick080` (f0's start), the
trainer's items of `sent_kanji`'s data (f0's and pres's build) in its order,
one a batch, dealt round-robin to σ 0.5–0.7 (uniform) / 0.8 / 0.9 / 0.95 ×
A / B, 100 items each (800); per item one forward, 8 probes on the box and
8 off it, one backward each on the retained graph to `llm_adapter.embed`'s
output, summed over a row's occurrences in the item → one g per (item, row)
pair; 490–560 kana pairs and 150–175 kanji pairs per fit. Drift: band 0.8
refit at `sent_kanji_f0`'s and `sent_kanji_pres`'s rows on the same items,
ε, σ and probes. Jobs `20261007-212311-536ec1` (start, 51.6 min, 3.85 s /
item), `-9f5d16` (f0, 13.1 min), `-fac882` (pres, 13.3 min) →
`output/cjk_anima_reseed/probe_jl/{start,f0,pres}/` (`g.pt`; `read.json`,
`read_pair.json`).

**bf16 cannot read this.** Against an fp32 forward + backward of the same
item (SDPA, bf16 weights cast — exact), the bf16 gradient on `embed`'s
output at the row positions has cos 0.56–0.61 (FA2) / 0.26–0.48 (SDPA),
solo or batched; the whole-sequence gradient 0.65–0.85; the bf16 forward is
1 % off; TF32 is 18–24 % off; fp32 repeats exactly. Under bf16 the batch
does not leak (item 1's probe gives item 0 exactly 0), but a compiled B = 4
graph's first backward differs from its later ones by ~50 % (calls 2–4
agree to 2–4 %). M is a second moment, so bf16 noise would sit in it as
E[e eᵀ]: the probe runs fp32 / SDPA / eager, batch 1, and asserts at its
first item that the hook's g matches autograd on `raw` and that a repeated
backward is exact (both 0). Not measured: what this does to the trainer's
step (its gradient is a structured residual summed over a batch, not a
random probe at one position) — the bf16 compiled reads in `probe_geom` /
`probe_pres` / `probe_scene` carry it in their per-draw noise, and
`probe_pres.grad` takes three gradients off one compiled B = 4 graph.

Two readings of the same g: **none** (M as defined) and **pair** (each
pair's g over its RMS across probes and both masks — the pairs' size tail
off, their in / out split and directions kept). One-row items (a single
glyph) carry |g| up to 2–4× the rest and weigh up to 4–16× in M.

## Verdict

- **Probe 0 stops** by the rule as written: A / B overlap 0.21–0.50 at
  k 16. Pair-weighted, kana reach 0.69–0.80 at σ ≤ 0.9 and still climb with
  the sample, so the lens is estimable for kana at a larger budget; kanji
  are not at this one.
- **The projection arm is not run, and a larger fit would not save it.**
  The arm projects row steps onto the high-λ subspace to hold the page at
  L_pres's σ 0.8–0.9. There the cross-fit λ is ~1–2 (none at 0.9): the
  rows have no direction that moves the box without the page where the
  page is at risk. Such directions exist at σ 0.5–0.7, where the page
  already sits at its smallest share of the row's effect.
- **What the lens adds**: the σ ladder of the box's share
  (1.3–1.9 → 0.06–0.10), and a reading of moves that needs no target —
  L_pres pushes the rows toward the page, through one shared vector in the
  most page-sensitive directions; the data term toward the box. As a
  predictor of the ruler's reads it adds nothing over the move's size.
- **Under a latent-cell metric.** The probe sits on the prediction's
  cells, so M_out counts any change outside the box; λ ~1 at σ 0.8–0.9 is
  "no direction moves the box without moving the page's cells". A page
  metric on what the ruler scores (`en_match`: the scene's identity, e.g.
  PE features of the out-box x̂₀) is not tried; it is the one change that
  could reopen the projection arm.
- **bf16**: the line's gradient probes ran in bf16, where the per-position
  row gradient is at cos 0.3–0.6 to fp32's; their per-draw numbers carry
  that. The trainer's own step is not measured here.
