# freeswap — M2, the template where the base chooses the canvas (2026-10-16)

`proposal_template.md` P0b. M1 (`swapword_2026_10_16.md`) read held-out
training items, where the canvas is already in x_σ, and was blind to the
template. M2 runs the same word / swap split on the EN trajectory instead.

Probe: `probes/freeswap.py`. Daemon job `20261016-140143-5f2033`, 15 min.
Results (gitignored): `results/20261016-1416-freeswap-all/` (`table.md`,
`rows.json`, sheets of the decoded caption-mean x̂₀ at σ 1.0 / 0.95, seed 0).

## Set-up

- **x₀**: the EN render (`, english text. English text reads as "hi".`) of
  p00 / p01 × seeds 0 – 5, drawn again on identity's render path. It
  reproduces `OUT/commit/EN/` at |Δpx| 0.000. **x_T** is that seed's step-0
  latent, and x_σ = (1 − σ)x₀ + σx_T at σ ∈ {1.0, 0.95, 0.9, 0.85}.
- **Captions**: `hira_cold_hp`'s 13 `read` words plus K = 3 swaps each (M1's
  rule, within the word's script).
- **Tables**: the floor's table, with the 81 hiragana rows taken from the arm.
  `ref` has the hiragana and katakana rows at the pack. The katakana words
  read the floor's (`retrain_kana`-trained) rows in every arm.
- **Reads**: on the generation path's x̂₀ (g = 4, v_u shared):
  - `M` / `S`: the caption mean and the rest of x̂₀(arm) − x̂₀(ref).
  - `T` / `V`: their energy, in std-scaled latent units.
  - Pixel shifts of decode(caption-mean x̂₀), arm − ref. These are M's pixel
    effect. `pale` = −Δsat.
  - `div`: the true caption's x̂₀ variance over the 6 seeds, against ref's.
    `collapse` = 1 − div.
- **Gate** (σ 1.0 / 0.95 pooled, per hiragana word):
  - the floor above x2 and above sball_lr3 on ≥ 8 / 9 words each;
  - `up` above `hp`;
  - the katakana words tie.

## What it says

1. **By the prespecified gate, M2 fails.** The floor separation holds on
   `pale` (8 / 9 against both cold arms, strong slot-paired), but the
   positive control does not: `up` does not read above `hp` (4 / 9).
2. **The positive control was not one for this read.** `up`'s known habit is
   swapping scene parts. It does read highest on `T` (7 / 9 over hp), on
   Δwhite and on σ = 1 div (1.45). It does not read on desaturation, which
   is the plain-type look. This is a post-hoc reading: it explains the
   failure, it does not overturn it.
3. **The floor's M carries a desaturating shift at σ = 1, and almost nowhere
   else.** At 1.0 it is 3 – 9× the cold arms'; from 0.9 down it is gone. On
   the sheets, every arm brightens the σ = 1 mean image against ref. The
   cold arms also add a dark red / coloured mass in the upper corners, and
   the floor stays an even pale. retrain_kana's katakana rows read paler
   still (0.0150). So rows trained on retrain_kana's data push the first
   step toward a pale, colourless page whatever the word. That is word-free,
   so it sits in M.
   - This leans against negswap's "template in S".
   - Caveat: the swaps use the same arm's hiragana rows, so a style shared by
     every hiragana row of an arm is counted in M even if it is glyph-bound.
     The two reads are not strictly in conflict.
4. **No seed collapse at one step.** commit's step-3 diversity drop
   (retrain_kana 0.40 / 0.58 of EN) does not show at σ 1.0 / 0.95. Here the
   rows raise seed variance, and the floor least. The collapse forms over
   the first steps, not in the first prediction.
5. **‖M‖ is not the template.** Its magnitude does not separate the floor.
   Its direction (desaturation) does.

## What it does to the plan

- Per § 2, M2 is not the gate meter, and the template stays read on renders
  (commit's cross-word variance, `white` / `sat` in the glyph box).
- **`pale` at σ = 1 is worth carrying into P1 as a secondary read**, since
  L3 is its proper control. L3b (the large high-σ glyph on flat white pages)
  should raise it over L3a. If it does, the read is validated on a template
  we put there ourselves. If it does not, drop it.
- L4 (an anchor on M at σ ≥ 0.9) is not ruled out by this: the floor's
  template has a word-free part at σ = 1. That is the opposite of what
  negswap leaned to. L3 stays first.
