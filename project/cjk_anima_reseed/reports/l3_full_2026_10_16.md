# L3 at 150 a row — the large glyph at high σ in a scene, against sball_lr3 (2026-10-16)

The L3 lever moved here from `proposal_template.md` § 4. The micro pair
(`l3_micro_2026_10_16.md`) was null at 50 a row. This run takes L3a to the
full 150 a row and reads it against `hira_cold_sball_lr3`'s end. L3b was
stopped part way (user 10-16, below).

## Why L3

- `probes/canvas.py` (`results/20261016-1327-canvas/`): 18.8 % of
  `retrain_kana`'s hiragana glyph occurrences sit on a flat / grid page at
  σ ≥ 0.8 (80 px median). For hp the figure is 0.8 %, for mix 0.4 %.
- retrain_kana is the template bearer, and 81 % of its glyphs are in scenes.
  So canvas variety overall is not the marker; a plain page at the σ where
  the canvas is decided is.
- `motivation2.md` § 7.1 has retrain_kana's identity riding on those same
  σ 0.7 – 0.9 large items. Identity and template may come together.
- The lever keeps the high σ and the large glyph and takes the plain page
  away:
  - **L3a**: the large glyph at high σ in a scene. The question is whether
    identity rises over sball_lr3 while the plain-type template stays at the
    cold arms' level.
  - **L3b**: the same tier on flat white pages, the template's direct test.
    If L3b brings the plain-type habit and L3a does not, the template is
    canvas × σ.
- Risk: `motivation2.md` § 6 has `kana_big` (a 65 px grid at σ 0.65 – 0.8)
  not closing the gap. That was a grid on flat canvases, at 0.8.
