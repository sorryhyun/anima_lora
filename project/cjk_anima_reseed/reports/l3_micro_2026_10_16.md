# L3 micro — the large glyph at high σ, in a scene or on a white page (2026-10-16)

`proposal_template.md` P1. The question is whether retrain_kana's plain-type
template comes from canvas × σ: the large glyph on a plain page at the σ
where the canvas is decided (`swapword_2026_10_16.md`, canvas exposure).

## Arms

Both arms are `hira_cold_sball_lr3` with bubble1_52's share (11.1) moved to
a new tier:

- **L3a** (`hira_cold_l3a`, `bubble1_72`): font px 64 – 96 (ink median 72),
  σ 0.65 – 0.9, coloured scene bubbles only (`mono` off), the ink centred in
  its bubble or sign (`pose`, user 10-16).
- **L3b** (`hira_cold_l3b`, `lone_72`): the same px and σ, the glyph alone on
  a flat white page (ink median 67).

Design notes:

- **Why bubble1_52 and not grid_44** (user 10-16): grid_44 carries 6.3
  glyphs an item, 63 occurrences a row and 15 % of all. Swapping it for a
  one-glyph tier would cut a row's ≥ 40 px occurrences from ≈ 77 to ≈ 24.
  Swapping bubble1_52 (one glyph) keeps every row's occurrence count.
- **Builds**: own builds. The grid and lone tiers draw as hira_cold_hp's.
  bubble1_32 and bubbleN are a new draw. L3a and L3b share every item but
  the new tier (7 200, checked).
- **Scene supply**: large bubbles are scarce. bubble1_72 uses 201 scenes,
  against bubble1_52's 426, after widening fill to 0.4 – 0.95 (132 scenes
  at 0.5 – 0.8). Part of L3a's items are glyphs on a held white sign.
- **Schedule**: the full 150-a-row schedule, stopped at 4050 (`--max_steps`),
  so the read is against `hira_cold_sball_lr3_s4050`. Train jobs
  `20261016-143957-da3d8d` / `-1a3f8e`, 28 min each.

Reads:

- identity grid (`results/20261016-1550-identity-l3_micro/`). It now also
  reads render `white` / `sat`, over the image and in the glyph box
  (`identity.plain`).
- freeswap at σ 1.0 / 0.95 (`results/20261016-1602-freeswap-l3_micro/`,
  `--arms` / `--pairs`).

## What it says

1. **At 50 a row, neither arm moves identity.** Words are 1 – 2 / 18 and
   singles 14 / 24 for all three cold arms. The high-σ large glyph in a scene
   (L3a) buys nothing over sball_lr3 at this strength.
2. **L3b does not bring the plain-type habit.** No read puts it above L3a:
   `pale` leans the other way, and box white is lower. By eye
   (`sheets/sheet_word_*`), every cold arm writes coloured title type in a
   top band and keeps the scene. L3b writes no thin dark type on white.
   L3a adds a second junk line on p01 (もむせ, よしし), and so does s4050
   (違うさん).
3. **The render box-white read is not a template meter.** The floor, the
   template bearer, reads *lower* than s4050 (box white on words 0.31
   against 0.59, slots 3 / 15, p 0.01). The cold arms write in white bands
   more often. Use it only next to a sheet.
4. **The test was underdosed against what it tries to reproduce.**
   - retrain_kana had 18.8 % of its hiragana occurrences on a flat / grid
     page at σ ≥ 0.8, and 18.0 % were grids at ≥ 0.9.
   - L3b's lone_72 is 899 one-glyph items, 2.6 % of occurrences, in σ 0.65 –
     0.9 and at 50 a row.
   - So L3b's null rules out "a little high-σ plain page makes the
     template", not canvas × σ at retrain_kana's dose.
5. **L3 against s4050 is confounded by the resample.** Both L3 arms read
   paler than s4050 at σ 1 by slot (+0.0030, p 5e-6; +0.0018, p 0.002), and
   that shared part is the new draw of bubble1_32 / bubbleN they share.
   Only L3a against L3b is clean.
