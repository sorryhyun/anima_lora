# swapword — a row's effect split by the word (2026-10-16)

The question (user 10-16) is glyphs memorized, templates not. The commit
report (`commit_2026_10_16.md`) closed on this: to meter it, read what does
not change when the word changes. This probe does that on one-step
predictions, with no sampling.

Probe: `probes/swapword.py`. Results (gitignored):
`results/20261016-1304-swapword-all/` (`table.md`, `rows.json`). Daemon job
`20261016-121514-bc38e9`, 49 min.

## Set-up

- **Items**: `hira_cold_hp`'s table drawn again at `table.SEED = 1`, 2.5 % of
  every tier, so 202 items (grid 100, lone 21, bubble1 40, bubbleN 41), in
  `OUT/swapword/heldout_s1/data`. Every image is new. 63 / 202 of the strings
  appear in the training set, mostly singles.
- **Swaps**: K = 3 a item. Every quoted glyph is replaced by another of the
  81, none of them the item's own glyphs; the same length and layout clause,
  and the same swaps for every arm.
- **Tables**: `seed_retrain_0930` with the 81 hiragana rows taken from the
  arm (so arms differ in those rows only). `ref` is the same table with the
  81 at the pack (Δ 0). There are 17 cold arms and saves.
- **Read**: per item × σ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9}, one x_σ shared by every arm.
  d(c) = σ · (v_arm(c) − v_ref(c)) is the rows' x̂₀ shift under caption c.
  `M` = mean over c (holds whatever the word), `S` = d − M (follows the word).
  - `T_in` / `T_in_lo` / `V_in`: ‖M‖², ‖blur₅(M)‖² and ‖S‖² in the box.
  - `rho` = T_in / (T_in + V_in).
  - `I` = L_wrong − L_true in the box (the correct word's margin).
  - `G_wrong` = L_wrong(ref) − L_wrong(arm) (what the rows buy with the wrong
    word).
  - `T_out` / `G_out`: the same outside the dilated box.

## What it says (revised after the fable review, 10-16)

1. **The word-specific side reads singles, not words.** On the run ends,
   only the single read survives. The word correlation over all 17 points
   is mostly "later saves are better on both". There is a counterexample:
   x2 from s12150 to the end raises `I` 0.050 → 0.053 while word exact falls
   12 → 9 (cer 0.278 → 0.364). So `I` cannot name a step budget.
2. **`G_wrong` falls as a run trains.** An early save's rows help even a
   wrong word (hp s4050 +0.0070); by the end that gain is about 0. `rho` falls
   with every save, but its floor is structural (1 / (K + 1) = 0.25 at K = 3),
   so the 0.588 – 0.612 spread is a 2 % effect. No save shows the word-free
   share rising.
3. **The word-free low-pass term tracks singles; whether it is template is
   undecided.** `T_in_lo` goes with single exact and against single dup. A
   row that makes the model draw one large centred glyph would also do that,
   and that habit is itself a template (the floor's, `hira_cold` § 4.2). The
   two highest σ 0.9 `T_in_lo` are the floor (0.0071) and `up` (0.0072), both
   arms with known layout habits. The floor's σ 0.9 lead is all on grids
   (0.0119 against sball_lr3's 0.0114; bubble and lone tie). The probe cannot
   separate text-ness from template, so the low-pass in-box anchor stays
   untested, not refuted.
4. **The gate fails: M1 is blind to the template.** On held-out items the
   floor sits mid-pack on T_in, T_in_lo and rho. The canvas is already in
   x_σ, and the template is a choice made where the canvas is free. Per
   glyph (71 rows with at least 3 held-out items), no M1 read follows a row's
   template exposure in `retrain_kana`'s data (`probes/canvas.py`, below).
   floor − sball_lr3 `T_in_lo` at σ 0.9 against the row's share of
   flat / grid occurrences at σ ≥ 0.8: ρ +0.14 (p 0.24). An arm that never saw
   that data shows the same weak slope (+0.11), so what slope there is
   belongs to the glyph, not the training.
5. **The floor's render lead is not in one-step identity.** Its `I` is
   mid-pack while it beats every cold arm on renders. Its lead is in the
   trajectory (dup, slot count).
6. **A weak lead: the word decided at σ 0.9 goes with repeats.** `I` at σ
   0.9 correlates with single dup (+0.55) over all 17 points, but against word
   exact it is 0.00 on the ends. Not a basis for an arm on its own.

## Canvas exposure (`probes/canvas.py`, CPU, `results/20261016-1327-canvas/`)

`retrain_kana`'s images are gone, so its read is from the records. For each
hiragana glyph occurrence: its canvas (lettered into a scene's bubble, a grid
cell, or a flat page) and the upper edge of its σ band.

| data | hiragana glyph occurrences | on a flat / grid canvas at σ ≥ 0.8 (px median) | scene occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|
| retrain_kana | 36 976 | **18.8 %** (80 px; grid ≥ 0.9 18.0 %) | 81.2 % |
| hira_cold_hp | 34 490 | 0.8 % (44 px) | 23.9 % |
| hira_cold_mix | 30 395 | 0.4 % (44 px) | 65.5 % |

The template bearer has the more varied canvases overall (81 % scenes), but
a fifth of its glyphs sit on a plain page at σ 0.7 – 0.9, at 80 px. The cold
arms put their plain pages at σ ≤ 0.8 and small glyphs. That is where the
canvas is decided (layout ≥ 0.9, `motivation2.md` § 1). It is also where
retrain_kana's identity came from (`motivation2.md` § 7.1: identity rode on
the σ 0.7 – 0.9 large items). Identity and template may ride on the same
items. On the pixels (hp / mix only, 3 000 items each), the "plain"
occurrences (dark neutral ink on a pale neutral ground) are 33 % for hp and
28 % for mix (bubble1 ≈ 0.46 – 0.49, bubbleN_18 ≈ 0.15), and canvas entropy is
1.16 / 1.49 bits a row. Plain-canvas share alone does not mark the template
bearer. Plain canvas *at high σ* does.

Caveats: one held-out draw (seed 1, 202 items). A T5 / Qwen caption with a
random swapped string is not a word, so L_wrong also carries Qwen-side
nonsense. The ref is subtracted under the same caption, so `d` cancels the
Qwen side; `I` does not.
