# commit — when the render leaves the EN scene, read off the trajectory (2026-10-16)

The reseed's motivation was retrain_kana's plain-text habit: a quoted kana word
drawn as thin black / grey type on a pale band or a white box, the same look
whatever the seed. The question (user 10-16) is whether that habit is a
memorized template, and whether the cold arms carry less of it as their
strength grows (`hira_cold_sball_lr3`, 150 / row → `_x2`, 300 / row). The aim
it serves: train identity as hard as the row will take while the row learns
no template or canvas, so that glyphs may be memorized and layouts may not.

Lens: Kim et al., "How Diffusion Models Memorize" (arXiv 2509.25705,
SD 1.4, ε-pred, DDIM; PDF and text extract at the repo's
`_archive/paper/how_diffusion_models_memorize_2509.25705.{pdf,txt}`). Their claim: a memorized prompt's cond prediction
carries the training image x and the uncond prediction does not, so at t = T
CFG gives x̂₀ ≈ g·x. That overshoot cuts seed diversity in the first steps,
and the trajectories lock onto x. They also decompose x_t over (x, x_T)
against the schedule; how far w₀ runs ahead of its schedule predicts
memorization (r 0.92 against SSCD). Pack rows enter the cond branch only
(T5("") holds none), so a layout a row carries reaches the image as exactly
that cond-only injection.

Probe: `probes/commit.py`, on identity's render path (512², 28 steps, cfg 4,
routed). It hooks the `TrajStatsRecorder` site, which runs after the CFG
combine and before the step. Arms render `hira_cold_hp`'s 13 `read` words ×
p00 / p01 × seeds 0–5, captioned `<prompt>. Text reads as "<word>".`. EN
renders the shared refs' caption (`hi`) with the delta off, per prompt ×
seed. It reproduces `enref_p0{0,1}_s{0,1}` at mean |Δpx| 4.6–8.1. Results
(gitignored): `results/20261016-1154-commit-rk_vs_x2/`; renders
`OUT/commit/<arm>/`.

## Reads

All reads are in the latent scaled by the VAE's per-channel std, which is
linear, so the flow path x_t = (1 − σ)x₀ + σx_T holds. D = x₀(arm) − x₀(EN)
at the same prompt and seed (same x_T).

- `r[t]` = ⟨x̂₀ₜ(arm) − x̂₀ₜ(EN), D⟩ / ‖D‖²: the share of the final
  departure from the EN scene already present in the step-t estimate.
- `inj0`: the same projection of the step-0 x̂₀(cond) − x̂₀(uncond) gap
  (Wen et al.'s statistic, signed along D).
- `dev` = ‖D‖ / ‖x₀(EN)‖.
- `w0_ex` = Σₜ (w₀ − (1 − σ)), where x_t = w₀x₀ + w_Tx_T is fitted per step
  by least squares.
- `div`: mean per-dim variance of the step-k x̂₀ across the 6 seeds, divided
  by EN's at the same prompt.
- `white` / `ink` / `sat`: pixel fractions near white and near black, and
  mean saturation.

- **The departure at σ = 1 is the cond injection × g, by construction.**
  r0 / inj0 = 3.97–3.98 on every render. The uncond branch is identical for
  every arm and EN, so the paper's premise (uncond carries nothing of x)
  holds exactly here, where it fails for LoRA memorization (that residual
  sits in the uncond pathway, `docs/proposal/memorization_uncond_gap.md`).
- **Early commitment predicts the end.** Pooled Spearman: r7 ↔ dev 0.72,
  r3 ↔ dev 0.69, r0 ↔ white 0.54 (all p < 1e-4). Within a prompt, r0 ↔ dev is
  0.23 (p00) / 0.38 (p01). The prompt moves r0 more than any arm does (p00
  0.11, p01 0.34).
- **Strength did not raise the σ = 1 injection.** x2 sits below retrain_kana
  on r0 and inj0 (−6 %), and below its own 150 / row arm on r0 and dev.
  `w0_ex` is negative for every arm (w₀ trails its schedule), so the
  paper's overshoot signature does not appear on this path.
- **Seed diversity separates the arms.** At step 3, every arm's estimate is
  0.40–0.65 as diverse as EN's. retrain_kana is the lowest on both prompts,
  and x2 is above it on 7 of the 9 hiragana words (かんがえ 0.48 → 0.77,
  こうえん 0.48 → 0.71). The four katakana words, whose rows these arms
  share, read identically across arms, which is the control.
- **Sheets.** retrain_kana writes thin black / grey type on a pale band or
  white box (かなしい p01 s4, かんがえ p01 s0, てつだう p01 s0 / s4). x2 writes
  coloured bold title type and keeps the scene. It has more repeats and junk
  glyphs and an occasional bare pink bar, consistent with its word exact
  dropping 12 → 9 from s12150 to the end
  (`results/20261016-1120-identity-hira_cold_sball_lr3_x2`).

## What it says

- retrain_kana's habit is a **text-style template** (plain dark type on a
  pale band), not lost scenes. Every arm keeps the scene. The trace it leaves
  is lower seed diversity in the first steps: the same look whatever x_T.
- D spans the whole image and is mostly scene change, so `r` and `dev` do
  not isolate the template. On this meter, 150 → 300 / row shows no growth
  in what the rows inject at σ = 1.
- To meter glyphs-yes / template-no, read what does **not** change when the
  word changes. At a fixed prompt and seed, low cross-word variance of the
  early x̂₀ means a template; cross-word variance in the text region at
  mid σ, together with exact, is identity. Read across saves
  (`sball_lr3_s4050`, `_s8100`, `x2_s12150`, the ends), this gives the
  strength at which the template starts to rise. That needs `commit.py` to
  keep the per-render x̂₀ at a few steps (≈ 0.4 MB a render). Not run.

Caveats: the EN caption differs from the arms' in more than the quoted word
(`, english text. English text reads as "hi".`), so `div / EN` mixes the
caption change with the rows. Read it between arms, not against 1.
`white` counts p01's white bedsheets.
