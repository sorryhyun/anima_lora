# 1k on merged15: the 250-iteration gains do not hold at 1000 iterations (2026-11-16)

`turbo_1116_1355` is the shipped recipe of `configs/methods/turbo.toml` (v1 at 1000 iterations,
cosine 5e-5 → 5e-6, EMA 0.2 of the summed LR, `dm_first_step` on, static grid, cond-only L_CDM) on
the merged15 teacher, seed 42. It is `turbo_1116_0958` at 1000 iterations. Config
`configs/turbo_1116_1355.toml`, job `20261116-135536-2add48`, 92 min at about 5.4 s per iteration.

**Verdict: failed.** User's read of the renders: a clear failure. The EMA 1k crops tighter than the
250-iteration arm and draws one layout per prompt; Hub v3 on merged15 with no training stays the
closest 4-step result to the teacher on every probe number and in the renders. The two changes
adopted at 250 iterations (static grid on 11-14, cond-only L_CDM on 11-16) did not keep the 1k
student off the attractor `reports/warm_v3.md` describes.

## Training curves

50-iteration means against `0958` over its 250 iterations: the first 50 overlap (`dm` 0.274 / 0.274,
`rel_gap` 0.240 / 0.237). A burst at iterations 70–80 (`gn_student` 21–24, `cdm` and `dm` up with it)
passed under the clip at 1.0. `dm`, `rel_gap` and `cos` improved slowly through 400 (`rel_gap` 0.204,
`cos` 0.975 at 400, the level `0958` ended at with its LR near zero). `fake` rose from 0.11 to about
0.20 by 400 where `0958` stayed near 0.12. None of the curves shows the layout loss.

## What it means

- **A 250-iteration A/B does not predict the 1k student.** Both adoptions were read at 250
  iterations, where the cosine had already annealed; at 1k the same recipe lands at the control's
  sameness. Gains read at 250 need a 1k check before they ship.
- **`dm_first_step` off is unlikely to be the fix.** `turbo_1113_1309` turned it off from the v3
  start and took back 0.024 of the 0.103 rise in sameness (`reports/warm_v3.md`). `1112_v1.md`
  names it as the second run if diversity falls with length; that evidence came after it.
- **The warm start alone is not it either:** from v3 the v1 recipe regresses within 250 iterations.

What separates Hub v3 from this line (its snapshot `output/logs/turbo/20260828-024030/`):

Every student trained on merged14 or merged15 lands at the same sameness whatever its start,
`dm_first_step` or fake init; the one student that keeps the teacher's diversity was trained on
the base teacher. The current recipe has never run on the base teacher. That is the largest
untested difference, and the cheapest one to test: the recipe at 250 iterations on base v1.0,
probed against the base teacher's own sameness (about 27 min). If the base student keeps its
diversity, the merged teacher is the attractor and the route is v3's (distil on base, merge the
soup after). If it collapses too, the remaining differences (length, the turbo v1.1
start) are next. Not run.

Hub v3's run kept no intermediate checkpoints, so whether v3 also collapsed early and recovered over
its 10k iterations cannot be read. The EMA 250 / 500 / 750 of `1355` are saved and not rendered;
they would show where the 1k run turned.
