## 3. What the reads show

- **3 glyphs:** the gain is すごい. 一緒に and 気持ち read 0 on every arm:
  the first glyph renders (一, 気) and the rest is other text (一蹊にかウ,
  一発にだ僕は). This is the kanji-composition failure of proposal § 2.3,
  not exposure.
- **4 glyphs:** all four rise at × 3; もちろん 0 → 7 carries most of it
  (× 1 reads もるるん / ちちるん, × 3 もちるん / もちろん).
- **5 glyphs:** the head renders and the tail is lost or rewritten
  (こんに大は, ありまくめこしまますう, にたかうない). × 3 moves `near`
  8 → 13, and nothing to the whole string.

## 4. What it decides

  is not read; plan_2900 § 2 had B0 set C-p's budget too.
- Run B splits into `run0927_long_p3` (353, × 1) and `run0927_long_p45`
  (168, × 3), ≈ 9.3 h; C-p into `run0927_cold_p23` (513, × 1) and
  `run0927_cold_p45` (99, × 3). Each pair joins by `scale.py <out> merge`.
- 5-glyph pieces keep a sequence failure that budget does not fix. The
  kanji-bearing 3-glyph pieces are a line-mode question (proposal § 2.0 /
