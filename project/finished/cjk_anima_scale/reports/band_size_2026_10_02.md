# band × glyph size — the reads of 2026-10-02

A day that started as "the seed trained in the wrong bands" and ended as "the
seed has no item that could train above 0.7". Two reads ran (`kana_reband`,
the `--b0305` mirror), the cached P1 arms were re-read for glyph size, and a
third arm (`grid_small`) is training as this is written. Results and what
they changed; the reseed draft they feed is `../../cjk_anima_reseed/motivation.md`.

**Verdict.**
- **The seed's bands were right for its glyph sizes.** Every arm of record
  that produced identity had its glyphs inside the ceiling window of their px
  (`band_experiment_results.md` § 2); every arm that lost it moved the same
  px above that window. Today's `kana_reband` is one more of the second kind.
- **The ceiling table and the trajectory are two quantities, not a
  contradiction.** The ceiling is where a teacher-forced loss carries a caption
  gradient for a glyph of that px — training is teacher-forced, so it is the
  only thing a band can follow. The trajectory (`findings.md`) is where a row's
  vector is consumed at inference. A row has no σ input: the band chooses what
  gradient it sees, not where it acts.
- **No arm has trained a word of 48 px or more at a band that reaches 0.8.**
  That, not the band table, is the gap between the seed's word items
  (≤ 64 px, σ ≤ 0.7) and where a large banner's identity is committed
  (0.9–0.7).
- **The target is manga-size dialogue, not banners** (user, 10-02): text in
  speech bubbles of multi-panel and single-panel manga and illustrations —
  small. The large glyphs were for identity.
- **Identity does not need large glyphs.** `p1_cold` (9-28) trained 36
  hiragana rows cold on in-word items at 0.3–0.7 only, nothing above ≈ 40 px,
  and reads ≤ 1 edit 65 / 128. What the large lone tier buys is the lone
  single (official 29 → 82 / 144) and a larger rendered glyph (§ 4).
- **A σ gate does not yet give manga-size text.** The base above 0.8 with the
  seed rows below keeps the base's small bubbles and writes the word's glyphs
  into them, with repeats: 0–1 of 16 against the seed's 7 (§ 3).
