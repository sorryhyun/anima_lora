# stage_i — proposal § 2.3: which scene makes a good identity (2026-09-26)

Three identity recipes on 12 cold kanji the seed rows lack
(山 田 野 郎 太 道 場 星 空 天 地 小). Each arm is 800 items (the line's
items per row), the fixed trainer at 90 steps / row, and is read with its
rows + 0.5 · `v_line`. **Verdict: criterion 1 ranks the recipes I0 ≥ I1 ≫
I2. Criterion 2 is unreadable: every arm, and the mode reference, spells 0
of 96 on every word.** At 90 steps / row cold, only 3 of 12 kanji reach
official in any arm (天 小 田). The rows carry too little identity to
compose, so the "ties on 2 → `b0709` stays" rule is a tie at zero, not the
tie it was written for. Grid-only (I2) is clearly worst, so "native needs
scene" holds on cold kanji.

One job, `20260926-225310-57bd5c` (3 × 8.3 min train + 1 250 renders,
106 min). Script `experiments/stage_i/run_exp.py`; envelope
`experiments/stage_i/results/20260926-2253-gpu1/result.json`. Rows
`output/cjk_anima_scale/run0926_si_i{0,1,2}/`, read arms
`output/cjk_anima_scale/experiments/si_i{0,1,2}/native_stagei/`. The floor
keys (12 singles + 6 words, en) are in the seed dir's `native_stagei/`.

## 1. The arms (as built)

| arm | recipe | σ | ink px (p10–p90) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I0 | `b0709` as is: `scene_single` fill 0.7 + `grid_single` 1×1–3×3 | 0.7–0.9 | scene 53 (43–78), grid 103 (63–210) |
| I1 | `scene_single` at a target px, bubble fill 0.2–1.0 | 0.7–0.9 · 0.5–0.7 | 56 (48–65) · 36 (30–39) |
| I2 | `grid_single` 3×3 / 2×3 at a target px | 0.7–0.9 · 0.5–0.7 | 57 (44–74) · 32 (26–38) |

The band law has no single-glyph window under 40 px at 0.7–0.9, so I1 and
I2 put half their items in a 0.5–0.7 single group, as Stage B's count tier
did.

## 2. Criterion 1: alone (en, 12 kanji × 16 = 192; gate off)

| | floor (pack rows) | I0 | I1 | I2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| official | 0 | **31** | 16 | 6 |
| contained | 4 | **59** | 54 | 21 |
| repeat | 0 | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| alone as a line (≥ 3 glyphs read) | 191 | **131** | 146 | 180 |

- Paired: I1 vs I0 official 5 / 20 (p 0.004), contained 22 / 27 (n.s.),
  line 34 / 19 (p 0.053). I2 vs I0 official 3 / 28, contained 6 / 44,
  line 51 / 2 (all p < 1e-5).
- **On cold rows the "line" metric reads the failure to render.** An
  untrained kanji row renders a line of other text in 191 / 192. Training
  lowers it by rendering the glyph instead. Here lower is better; on the
  warm kana of F1 / count_twin it was a layout cost.
- Per glyph (official / contained, I0 · I1 · I2): 天 10/15 · 3/5 · 1/1,
  小 11/14 · 8/13 · 0/4, 田 8/14 · 3/13 · 3/7, 山 2/14 · 0/5 · 2/5; 星 is
  I1's (0/7 vs I0 0/1). 郎 場 空 道 are 0 official in every arm.
- The sheet (`si_i0` / `si_i1` `sheet_天_en.png`): a rendered 天 is one big
  glyph, and in several renders the scene is replaced by a plain canvas. I1
  does not remove that.

## 3. Criterion 2: under the mode (en, 6 words × 16 = 96)

Exact, contained, ≤ 1 edit and `wdup` are **0 on every arm**, floor and
seed + 0.5 · `v_line` included (one `wdup` in I2). The reads are lines
of unrelated text (山田太郎 → 愒睙会戦, 坊薬坊 …). With 9 of 12 glyphs
never official alone, a word of them has nothing to compose. This is not
evidence that the recipes tie.

## 4. What it decides

- **Grid-only is out** (I2 loses on every singles metric), and the
  incumbent stays the reference for identity.
- **I1 vs I0 is open on criterion 1** (contained ties, official and line go
  to I0) and **unread on criterion 2.**
- **Stage I as specified is underpowered on cold kanji at 90 steps / row.**
  Reading criterion 2 needs rows that render their glyph alone first. The
  obvious next arm is I0 vs I1 at more steps / row (e.g. 3×, ≈ 25 min
  train each, the floor already cached; I2 dropped), read on the same keys.
  The alternative, warm rows, reintroduces the `b0709` confound the setup
  was built to avoid.
- The closing rule of proposal § 4 ("Stage I ties → `b0709` stays") is not
  triggered by a tie at zero.

## 5. At 3× budget (job `20260927-004911-3255ac`, envelope `…/20260927-0049-x3gpu`)

I0 and I1 were rerun with 3× items per row (2 400 per arm, same px
distributions) and 3× steps per row (270; 3 240 steps, 22 min per arm).
`--budget 3` scales both constants for this experiment only. I2 was
dropped.

**Criterion 1 (alone, / 192):**

| | I0 x1 | **I0 x3** | I1 x1 | I1 x3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| official | 31 | **58** | 16 | 48 |
| contained | 59 | **117** | 54 | 84 |
| repeat | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| alone as a line | 131 | **108** | 146 | 125 |

- The budget buys identity on cold kanji. For I0 x3 vs x1, contained is
  62 / 4 (p 2e-14) and official 37 / 10. Glyphs at 0 at x1 now render:
  道 7 / 10, 星 2 / 12, 地 3 / 10, 郎 3 / 7. 野 and 空 stay near 0.
- **I0 beats I1 at the same budget.** Contained 44 / 11 (p 9e-6), line
  16 / 33 (p 0.02), official 33 / 23 (n.s.). The px spread and small
  fill of I1 cost identity; I1 wins only on 山 and 道 official.

**Criterion 2 (the six words, + 0.5 · `v_line`, / 96):** exact,
contained and ≤ 1 edit are still 0 on both arms. Read more loosely:

| | floor | mode ref | I0 x1 | I1 x1 | I2 x1 | I0 x3 | I1 x3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| word's first glyph in a read | 1 | 0 | 12 | 5 | 7 | **25** | 15 |
| ≥ 2 of its glyphs in order | 0 | 0 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| mean share of its glyphs read | .01 | .01 | .10 | .04 | .07 | .16 | .08 |

- **The mode does not compose these rows.** A word renders its first
  glyph (山田太大幸恥, 太大右側, 道) and then unrelated text. That is the
  seed's pre-mode "one big first glyph" shape. It does not improve with
  identity: I0 x3 has 小 15 / 16, 山 15, 田 14 contained alone, and
  小山田 still reads 0 in order. On kana, the same 0.5 · `v_line` took the
  seed's held-out words from ≤ 1 edit 11 to 80 / 160
  (`f1_line_2026_09_26.md` § 5).
- So criterion 2 cannot pick between identity recipes here. `v_line` was
  learned from kana donors on seed rows. The seed's kana rows are
  `b0709`-trained too (single-glyph contexts only), and they compose under
  it. So the gap is kanji vs kana, or row maturity (the seed's rows trained
  far longer than 270 steps / row). The likely controls are a kanji-trained
  line mode, or `v_line` on the seed's own kanji rows (warm, if the seed
  holds kanji words).

**What it decides (replaces § 4's open ends):**
- **`b0709` (I0) stays the identity recipe, on criterion 1.** The
  alternatives lose at both budgets. That is a verdict on criterion 1, not
  the "every candidate ties on 2" close.
- **Cold kanji need more than 90 steps per row** (the line's
  `STEPS_PER_VOCAB`, set on warm pieces). 3× doubles contained and official.
- **New open item:** the line mode does not compose kanji rows (≥ 2 glyphs
  in order ≤ 3 / 96 on every arm). Kanji vs kana, or row maturity.
