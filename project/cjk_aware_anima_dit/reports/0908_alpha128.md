# α=128 re-run of the plain-vs-OCR pair — the instrument opened up (2026-09-08)

**Status: RENDERED, grading open.** Blind set `s17_OCR128_vs_PLAIN128` pushed,
24 pairs, unfilled.

## Why

s16 came back flat on every automated readout, and pulling the judge json for
**every arm this line has ever run** showed why that was uninformative: all of
them sit at `cos→base` 0.975–0.986 and `cos→sincos` 0.903–0.912, with the
spread between two *training seeds of the same arm* (C10: 0.9840 / 0.9865 /
0.9793; C9 vs C9s2: 0.9857 / 0.9787) larger than any between-arm gap ever
measured. At α/r = 1 and lr 2e-5 the adapter barely moves the base, so no
caption policy has room to express. This re-runs the s16 pair with **one
change** — `network_alpha` 32 → 128 (α/r = 4) — to test whether the line's
"everything ties" is an underpowered-instrument artifact.

Configs `cjk_unmask_{plain,ocr}_a128.toml`; latents and TE caches reused
bit-identically (`--skip_cache`), `use_repa` still false as in every C arm,
seed 42, 8 epochs / 2808 steps, 27:36 per arm.

## What α actually changed

| ckpt | α | ‖ΔW‖_F | ‖A,B‖_F |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `cjk_unmask_plain` | 32 | 2.2817 | 60.04 |
| `cjk_unmask_plain_a128` | 128 | 8.4165 | 61.12 |
| `cjk_unmask_ocr` | 32 | 2.3177 | 60.09 |
| `cjk_unmask_ocr_a128` | 128 | 8.8852 | 61.57 |

**The learned factors are the same weights** (‖A,B‖ +1.8 %) and ‖ΔW‖ scaled
3.7× — i.e. essentially all of the α/r = 4. The ~16× effective-step argument
(both A and B gradients scale by s) **did not materialise, because Adam
normalises it away**: α is a pure output-scale knob here, not a learning-rate
knob. Worth remembering before reaching for α as an LR proxy again.

## What it did to the images

PE-Spatial pooled cosine, matched (arm, seed, row), against rulers already on
the table:

| contrast | n | mean cos | min |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| different render seed (unrelated image floor) | 8 | 0.9347 | 0.8889 |
| **OCR vs PLAIN @ α128** | 24 | **0.9794** | 0.9519 |
| α32 vs α128 (OCR) | 24 | 0.9819 | 0.9477 |
| α32 vs α128 (PLAIN) | 24 | 0.9827 | 0.9420 |
| LoRA vs base, same seed (α32 PLAIN) | 24 | 0.9837 | 0.9253 |
| **OCR vs PLAIN @ α32** (the s16 contrast) | 24 | **0.9862** | 0.9187 |

Two things fall out:

- **The metric has range** — 0.935 is "different image", so 0.98 is "same
  image, perturbed", not saturation. `cos→base` looked dead across arms
  because every arm *is* the same tiny perturbation, not because the ruler is
  broken.
- **α widened the caption contrast.** The two arms moved from 0.9862 apart to
  **0.9794** apart — past the "α32 vs α128" perturbation itself and past
  LoRA-vs-base. The caption difference is now the *largest* non-seed effect on
  the table, where at α32 it was the smallest.

Judge and spam, for the record (both still flat, as expected — they were flat
between every arm this line has run):

| arm | adherence prob | recall | cos→base | cos→sincos | cells w/ box | boxes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OCR128 | 0.7535 | 0.8951 | 0.9785 | 0.9060 | 12 / 24 | 23 |
| PLAIN128 | 0.7457 | 0.9023 | 0.9816 | 0.9056 | 12 / 24 | 22 |

**Spam is equal and did not blow up at 3.7× ΔW** — the prediction that PLAIN
would reproduce the 0901 arm-B text spam at a stronger operating point is
**not** borne out on the box counts (12/12 cells, 23/22 boxes, vs 10/11 and
15/26 at α32). Whatever produced arm B's spam, adapter magnitude is not it.
