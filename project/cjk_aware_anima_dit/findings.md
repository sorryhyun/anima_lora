The AnimeText basis itself moved twice under user label passes — 619 → 617 SFX
(three `＾＾＾` scribbles dropped, one blank row joined), speech 294 → 293,
chrome 35 → 39, `checked` 70 → 86. Every `/ 619` figure in the archived
reports and plans is pre-revision. The ledger below is on 617.

### 2. The eval key was re-based (2026-09-08 00:12, `acd41d72`)

`exact_key` gained the ellipsis fold (each dot run → one `…`), the eval half of
the O4e guard fix. Every row measured before that scores `・・・`, `...` and
`…` as three different reads — worth up to +495 lines on COO speech,
+37…+154 on COO SFX, +4…+9 on the sincos gate.

`ocr/rescore_eval.py` re-derives `exact` for every stored `output/ocr/eval/*.jsonl`
from its own `pred_norm` + `text` columns (CPU, no model re-run). The ledger
below is the output of that script — every row on one key and one basis. The
archived `reports/ocr_eval*.md` files were never regenerated (`--write` not
run), so a report file and this ledger will disagree by exactly the fold.

### 3. Harness and jitter floors

- The VL readers carry a ±1-line batching jitter at different `--bs`; a
  1-line move is not a result.
- The same weights read 312 (`eval_sfx.py`) and 316
  (`context_margin_sweep.py`, pad 0.12) on the same 617 rows with the same key.
  Compare rows produced by one harness.
- On either basis the sincos speech / chrome rows are not an accuracy
  metric: their `text_hand` is PP-OCRv6 record text on almost every row, so
  reading `♡` correctly scores as a miss (§ O3 label audit).

## Settled — ext rows, the pack, and the DiT (read before any address arm)

- **Row content is inert for LoRA training; rows must exist.** A content-free
  table of the trained pack's geometry trains the same LoRA as the trained
  pack, on wrong captions (arm R; s13 ISO1-vs-C9 23–20) and on corrected ones
  (s21 SHIPRAND-vs-SHIP 17–18, 13 ties); rows > no rows (C9 > P). The
  isotropic block is the OCR-route default on cost, not quality. `k_norm`
  strips row scale on the K path, so norm / gain arms on the table are dead.
  (history § D0, § s21)
- **Those verdicts were measured on a DiT that could not read the rows**
  (whole-page LoRA, text a few % of pixels, ~60 exposures per row). They say
  nothing about what a row *can* carry: a frozen 2B DiT with a frozen adapter
  draws the requested hiragana from a rows-only ext-row delta (8 kana →
  9/16 singles, 24 kana → 24/36 with the σ band; kanji atoms 10/12 on seed 0).
  Never spend a body LoRA on glyph shape. (history § Wake;
  `reports/wake_w0_w2_2026_09_13.md`)
- **Identity is decided at σ ≈ 0.8** (same-noise diffusion classifier: chance
  at σ ≤ 0.65 and at 0.95). Train rows in σ 0.7–0.9, not `t_max 0.6`.
  Resolution below 512² is dead — the base cannot spell there.
- **Free rows do not scale.** Every character is its own inversion, rows
  interfere at matched exposure, balanced batches cannot cancel layout (the
  FM loss is a batch mean), and trained addresses are near-orthogonal random
  directions in row space with one shared "big glyph on a blank canvas" mode.
  The address is bound to the training clause (`Japanese text reads as "…"`),
  a Japanese-language clause is dead, and the layout mode wipes a scene prompt
  about half the time. The way out is an encoder from glyph shape to row
  delta whose output ships as a vocab pack (`plan_wake.md` W2d).
- **Composition is not a rows problem.** Kana combos render the first glyph;
  kanji combos fuse into one character-like glyph (品休 → 和) and composite
  kanji split into their parts (森 → 木林): the DiT composes at the component
  level in pixels and never learned token → position binding. That is
  DiT-side, ext-gated work (`plan_wake.md` W3).
- **EN safety holds by construction only for row deltas** (the hook touches
  ext positions; EN 24/24 on every arm). An adapter LoRA is not safe (24 →
  21/24) and needs an ext gate.
- **D1 shipped**: deterministic isotropic block + quote-route partition
  (`library/anima/ext_vocab.py`, `make_random_pack.py --mode iso |
  iso-partition`, native mean row norm 212.165), `ss_ext_pack_sha` stamped on
  every LoRA, `load_dit_model` warning on a stamped LoRA with no pack. The
  rule is inert unless both `iso` and `route.quotes` are present. Adapter node
  3.10.0 exists locally, never published.
- **Blind-set protocol.** Never chain blind sets (transitivity failed twice,
  s03/s04 and s12/s13) — pair the arms you compare; the seed-twin floor is
  the bar; graders have side habits (s14: 31/36 on side B). α is a pure
  output-scale knob (32 → 128 scales ‖ΔW‖ 3.7×, factors unchanged) and at
  α/r = 1 seed spread exceeds any between-arm gap ever measured; a more
  specified prompt is a *less* sensitive instrument; the spam tally changed
  units at D3 (detect-only boxes) and pre-D3 counts do not compare.

## Settled — captions

- Sentence-shaped OCR captions are the default (C10 vs C9ISOQ flat at the
  floor), the SFX sentence too (C11 flat). The shipped speech + SFX clauses
  are **neutral for image quality** on sincos: three blind reads, three ties
  (pooled 35–36 on 71 decisive). "Captions are load-bearing" rests only on the
  2026-09-01 spam result; moving it needs a text-requesting prompt grid or a
  second training seed per arm, not another render-only re-eval.
- Shipped caption rules (anime_tools): SFX deduped per sound unit
  (`sfx_key`), speech deduped on exact text (never `sfx_key`), a glyph floor
  of 16 px (`glyph_px = sqrt(w·h / len)`, not box area), all-VL records,
  `DROP_KINDS = {chrome}`. Every arm through C11 trained before the dedupe /
  floor existed — a rebuilt cache is not the corpus they saw.
- SAM3 `speech bubble` is not the speech / SFX signal (balloons on 34 / 97
  pages; as a veto it moved one line). Outside-a-balloon is not SFX either.
- Known miss: Latin text hallucinated as kana (`Zzz...` → `ててて…`) is the
  only Latin that reaches a caption; no text rule separates it.

## Settled — the SFX reader (B′, `anime_tools.ocr.sfx`)

- **The tower was the doujin gap**: tower frozen 110 → unfrozen 312 / 617.
  Every row under 130 on sincos has a frozen tower or no fine-tune.
- **In-domain and the doujin gate decouple, five arms running** (col100,
  col1500 swap, ×3 epochs, LP-FT, tower SSL): each moved COO by +1…+4 and
  left sincos flat or worse. The gate's top is a ~10-line band at ~50 % (B′
  312, shipped 311, records 317, col100 323, hayai v2.1.5 316); binomial SE at
  n = 617 ≈ 12 lines. Nothing separated them.
- **The headroom is on the label side.** B′'s misses: 160 near, 110 mid, 43
  garbage, 55 ♡-only; ♡ is in 497 / 617 gate rows and 337 / 77k COO train
  rows. Heart positives — synthetic, `ocr/synth_sfx.py`, unwritten — are the
  one open lever; append small, never swap. The Hub reader is v3 `b2_norm4`
  (heart as the single-token `♥` in the training target only; ♡-blind 354,
  B′ 365; the `♥`→`♡` fold had cost 49 reads). Do not pick a target spelling
  without checking the tokenizer.
- Closed, mechanism in history: LP-FT (locks in the COO prior before the
  tower moves; −48 on the gate); more epochs (buy COO only); colorized COO as
  replacement (−31, ≈ 2.5 σ — the grey originals are load-bearing; as a 1.6 %
  append it is inside 1 σ); label-free tower SSL (a pixel target collapses
  the tower, a feature target is stock by construction; −5); page context
  (more frame never beats the 12 % crop, oracle dialogue is below baseline);
  prompt hints / language tags; HunyuanOCR-1.5 (not a JA manga reader — half
  its miss is the Chinese prompt, the rest small kana); full FT (fp32 Adam
  states for 800 M do not fit 16 GB; bnb ships no cu132 build).
- Outside readers: hayai v2.1 is the best zero-shot COO SFX reader measured
  (78.2 %; base mix unauditable); v2.1.5 ties B′ on the gate at 1/6 the
  parameters and drops 20 points on Manga109 — a differently aimed
  checkpoint, and the real alternative if the reader must get cheaper. hayai
  throughput: one process, bs 16 (33.6 crops/s); bigger batches and more
  processes are both slower (launch-bound 4-beam decode).
- Decode guard: area-tied (`area / 16²`, floor 12), 80 new tokens;
  `normalize_read` folds dot runs to `…` and hearts to `♡` *before* the
  runaway test (the old guard ate the longest line on 64 pages); `has_script`
  does not count `ー っ ッ ゝ ゞ ヽ ヾ`.
- Training-data facts: the official COO split ∩ Manga109-s (74 / 7 / 6 books —
  never a private split); NFKC-fold + strip whitespace before tokenising or
  6 % of speech characters become `[UNK]`; 1 : 1 by count is 5.4 : 1 by
  characters — weight by tokens; `deskew_crop` must swap w / h past 45° (the
  O1 bug transposed every axis-aligned box); the VL loss needs left-padding +
  `logits_to_keep` (fp32 logits over the 103k vocab OOM). Manga109-s / COO
  crops live under `~/manga109s/`, never in-tree.

## Settled — detection

- `deepghs/AnimeText_yolo` replaces PP DB + VL Spotting + mask components:
  sincos masked-page floor 38 → 4, hand-SFX exact 44 → 63, precision 59 / 60
  on a hand pass. Defaults since D3: `detector="animetext"`, `reader="vl"`
  (detect-only engine; PP-OCRv6 stays as the explicit torch-free pair;
  `--mask_dir` needs `--detector ppocr`). GPL-3.0 weights / NC data: runtime
  download only, never bundled into the package, the trainer or a node.
- Facts: model and input size are a wash (l 640 = 26 ms / page); keep `inner`
  (drop a box holding ≥ 2 others), never `outer`; never `join_cjk` YOLO boxes;
  PP recognition on block boxes is unusable; bound the ORT CUDA arena
  (`kSameAsRequested`, `HEURISTIC`, `gpu_mem_limit`) or it grows to 15 GB and
  kills the next session.
- Two-detector merge rules (still true of any merge): match at IoU ≥ 0.3 or
  containment ≥ 0.5 or touching + sim ≥ 0.75 after `join_cjk`; gate
  page-caption hallucinations on area > 40 %; a second reader needs three
  guards, not one; keep joined-block boundaries as a space.
- D2 (animetext captions vs C11) was killed unmeasured: the flip roughly
  doubled SFX lines per page, and whether that trains cleaner or spammier is
  unknown. Any DiT arm on this shard trains on the animetext captions by
  default; compare against C11 with that caveat or re-run D2.

## Gotchas that cost time

- Only `ANIMA_`-prefixed env reaches a daemon job (`ANIMA_MANGA109S_ROOT`,
  `ANIMA_ANIMETEXT_ROOT`); the OCR stage by module needs explicit `--dst
  post_image_dataset/resized --ocr_dir post_image_dataset/ocr`.
- `build_mirror`'s symlinks fail sporadically on the ntfs3 volume — `os.link`
  or retry once.
- Wake probes: read the largest detector box, both readers; ext rows are
  Qwen-piece keyed (「いい」 is one row); the VAE takes [-1, 1]; clear
  `shared["conds_cache"]` on every delta-scale switch; rows lr 1e-3 in
  row-norm units (3e-3 walks off-manifold).
- The sincos speech / chrome labels are PP-OCRv6 record text — never judge a
  speech re-read on them.
- The archived `reports/ocr_eval*.md` were never regenerated on the new key;
  `ocr/rescore_eval.py` is the truth.

## Do not re-propose

Carried out of the archived plans (named beside each item; they live in
`_archive/cjk_aware_anima_dit/plans/`) so the list survives the gitignored tree.

- **Do not size or screen a KO/ZH pool with B′** — it misreads both as
  Japanese, and a B′-selected arm cannot then score B′ (plan2 K0's `zh` row is
  exactly that confound). Do not re-run K0's `zh` arm off a B′ screen.
- **Do not label KO/ZH from `JustANormalTinkerer/animetext-ocr`** — its reads
  *are* hayai's, so it is the voter, not a teacher, and its Korean carries no
  띄어쓰기 (plan2 K1).
- **Do not judge a KO/ZH arm on the Japanese gates alone**, and do not re-run
  K4 run 1 expecting a different Japanese number. Build K3 first (plan2).
- **Do not ship `vl16_pl_kozh` or `vl16_pl_20k`** — NC pseudo rows
  (plan_vl_respace, plan2 § Constraints).
- **Do not patch spacing at inference** (a word segmenter, a stock re-read of
  Latin lines) — the user chose the retrain 2026-09-10 (plan_vl_respace).
- **Do not judge a spacing arm on `exact_key` alone**; it is whitespace-blind
  by design and cannot see the regression (plan_vl_respace R1).
- **Do not reintroduce a whitespace strip in training targets** for any VL arm
  (plan_vl_respace, `whitespace_fixed.md`).
- **Do not pick a target spelling without checking the tokenizer first** — R5
  lost 49 heart reads by folding the single-token `♥` into byte-fallback `♡`
  (plan_vl_respace R5).
- **Do not judge a heart-slot change on the COO columns** — at 0.31 % heart
  rows they cannot see the axis; only the sincos gate can (plan_vl_respace R5).
- **Do not read an EasyControl-only floor as a pack verdict.** Its target
  stream is the frozen DiT and `cache_llm_adapter_outputs = true` keeps the
  `llm_adapter` out of the graph, so such an arm has **no trainable weight
  between an ext row and a pixel** (plan_render S5).
- **Do not compare a render CER to any `/ 617` or `/ 71` figure** in
  `findings.md` — different units, different task (plan_render).
- No prompt hints or language tags for the reader; no SSL-tower arms; no
  page-context margin sweep — all closed (`findings.md`, `eval.md`).
- Block-compile first on OOM for EasyControl at 768, not grad checkpointing
  (closed line `dynamo_limit_contextvar`); and every GPU step goes through the
  daemon — a bare background process dies at ~1 min.
