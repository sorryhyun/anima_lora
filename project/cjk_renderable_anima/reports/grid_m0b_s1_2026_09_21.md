Reads:

- **M0b is the pass M0 could not give.** At ≈ 69 scene draws per row singles
  alone barely start (5/36, native `swap` 1/64); the mix, with half of those,
  reads 13/36 and 11/64. The grid's draws are the cheap ones the full table
  lacks.
- **S1 is not a pass.** Held gain is under the +0.15 gate and the trained
  strings gained nothing — a held > trained gap has no mechanism, so the two
  together read as noise around ≈ +0.05. The cost is real: word items took
  half the single-cell grid draws (49 → 25 per unit) and `single` / native
  `swap` fell toward the singles-only arm.
- **The count and layout of a word do move** (あたしの → あとししの, わっ！！ →
  わえ!!, んんっ？ → んん?): length and punctuation land, identity does not —
  the same split `sent_run.md` item 10 found for the sentence pass.
- **The arm asked its question too early.** With rows at 7–13/36 there is no
  identity for a word cell to combine; piece hit is bounded by it. A word-cell
  read needs learned rows: warm-start both the single-cell and the word-cell
  mix from `rows_step1_0920_s53k` (`--init_rows … --lr_warmup 500
  --init_anchor`), same 3 000 steps, same `row_dose.py` read. Not run.
- σ stayed 0.7–0.9 on the S1 arm (words the only variable); the plan's
  0.5–0.9 band is untested.
