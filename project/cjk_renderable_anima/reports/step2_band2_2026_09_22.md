the trainer's affine σ draw into `[t_min, t_max]` is remapped per item, through

by glyph count: 1 663 single-glyph items, 38 336 multi (`kind: single` means

## Reads

1. **The band is the lever.** One change from the 30 k run, a sixth of the
   steps: better on every eval group, tie on sub-exact, and the single-glyph
   native collapse mostly undone. What the 30 k run paid — draws at 0.7–0.9 for
   strings, where native multi-token text has no leverage — bought the
   run-length habit and the singles' native loss; removing them returned
   both.
2. **The σ-division works at the row level** (m̂ energy share 0.006 on
   multi rows): 0.35–0.7 training edits identity/order residuals and leaves
   the step1-trained high-σ direction alone. The user's reading — "step1's
   rows keep governing 0.7–0.9, step2 adds below it" — is what the drift
   decomposition shows.
3. **What is not yet bought**: exact match on sentence groups is still
   floor-saturated (0–1 / 16); multi-glyph native both-hit is 1/16; the
   run-length habit survives in ≈ 40 % of single-glyph natives. The rows
   moved 3 % of their norm — μ 0.3 + the cosine tail (warm_cos back to
   0.998) cap how far 5 k can go. The next knob is not the band.
