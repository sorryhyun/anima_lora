## Training

Both arms terminate normally. `warm_cos` 0.996 (plain) / 0.998 (pair) at
1 500; `warm_drift` peaks at 0.13 in both (plain step 500, pair step 250)
and decays with the lr to 0.064 / 0.042 — the anchor wins the cosine tail,
more so on the pair arm. Row norm 53 → 77 (plain) / 72 (pair), `rel` 0.35
/ 0.33 — plateau from step 500. Pair: loss 0.016 flat, `fm_plain` 0.09–0.12
(the plain-comparable number does not fall), `pres` 3–6 e-4, `ref_bias`
0.55 → 0.29 in 100 steps then flat. Plain: loss 0.10–0.12 flat. Neither
arm's FM number moves in 1 500 steps; the tables move (norm +40 %) and the
rulers separate anyway.

Sub-exact on the natives ties (+0.000, CI [−0.084, +0.084]).

`sheet_はい_en.png`, side by side: plain's five hits are a **large
stand-alone はい** — on a wiped white canvas (p01 s0), floating over the
scene (p02 s0), in a caption bar (p03 s1) — with pseudo-JA in a second box
on most renders. Pair keeps every scene (the en cos gain) and puts the
text where the EN reference put `hi`, at subtitle size, and there はい is
one piece of a pseudo-JA string (`僖まねにだい`, `翼ふたろ（はい）`,
`はまえのだい`). Both arms often bracket it `（はい）` — the EN-frame
quote pattern of the training captions. This is Δ1's `きじたと(か)` on a
2-glyph string: ΔFM removes the wipe and does not remove the co-text, and
"a fully addressed caption leaves nothing to invent" (S2's premise) does
not hold at this exposure — the caption *is* fully addressed (は and い
are kana rows; はい itself is no unit here, it occurs inside 4 training
strings) and the base still free-runs around it.

## Verdict

- **ΔFM on sentences: killed** by the plan's rule (paired lift below plain,
  not above plain's CI; co-text not below). Plain is the S2b loss.
- The sub-exact ruler ordered the arms where exact match (0/16 everywhere
  but one) could not, and the sheets agree with it — first use of the ruler
  as a decision, and it did not reward-hack (pair's extra JA glyphs did
  not raise its lift).
- The plain S2a arm at +0.131 pooled is above `sent_s24k_a1_s05` (+0.070,
  same recipe, 24 k, `merge_punct` seed) and below `sent2_s24k` (+0.161, no
  anchor) — on 1 500 steps, on a Δ1 seed, with `_held` groups in the pool.
  Not comparable step for step (different eval sets, group mix), but it
  says the rejudged-pools recipe is not behind the old one.

## Addendum — the μ 0.1 plain arm (same evening)

`rows_synth_s2a_s2a_plain_a01` (jobs `20260918-182332-23fe45` train + eval,
`…-2ff926` native): the S2a plain argv with `--init_anchor 0.1`, everything
else equal (Δ1 seed, `data_synth_s2a`, 1 500 steps, lr 1e-3). Asked whether
a looser anchor buys sentence lift, the way the no-anchor `sent2_s24k` pass
did at 24 k.

| | μ 0.3 (`s2a_plain`) | μ 0.1 (`s2a_plain_a01`) |
|---|---|---|
| `warm_drift` / `warm_cos` at 1 500 | 0.064 / 0.996 | 0.137 / 0.982 |
| row norm | 77 | 78 |
| sub-exact pooled (64) | **+0.131** | +0.086 |
| short / short_held / phrase / phrase_held | +0.198 / +0.174 / +0.107 / +0.047 | +0.076 / +0.155 / +0.110 / +0.002 |
| exact | 1/16 short, else 0 | 0 everywhere |
| native `en` hit both / 48 (はい /8) | **5** (5) | 2 (1) |
| native en cos / box IoU | 0.922 / 0.34 | 0.928 / 0.39 |
| native sub-exact | +0.091 | +0.111 (tie, CI [−0.089, +0.094]) |

μ 0.1 − μ 0.3 pooled **−0.046, 95 % CI [−0.118, +0.026], P(>0) 0.10**. The
rows moved twice as far from Δ1 and the sentence rulers did not follow;
はい drops 5 → 1. At this exposure (13 draws/row) the anchor is not what
holds sentence lift back — loosening it spends the seed's identity (the
sweep's singles cost) and buys nothing on sentences. `sent2`'s 2× lift at
μ 0 came with 24 k steps from a sentence table; whether that reproduces
is not a 1 500-step question, and S2b does not need it to pass.

**S2b anchor: μ 0.3** (the recipe of record). μ stays a knob to sweep on
S2b's own table if its lift lands under the gate, not before.
