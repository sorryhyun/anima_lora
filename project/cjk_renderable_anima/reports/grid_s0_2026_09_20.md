# grid S0 — k units per canvas vs one: the smoke (2026-09-20)

> 24 rows (`chars:あいうかきくこさしすたてとなにのはまもらるれん日`), raw pack, cold,
> plain FM, lr 1e-3 cosine, σ 0.7–0.9, batch 4. **A grid draw teaches identity
> (G500 14/36 vs A500 6/36 at equal steps) but a grid-only table plateaus well
> under singles: G1500 17/36 and native 13/64 against A3000's 29/36 and 44/64.**
> 500 → 1 500 grid steps bought count, not identity (glyph-in-read 22 → 22).
> Grid is not a replacement for the singles recipe; whether it *adds* to it is
> the mix arm's question (`../plan_grid.md`).

Reads:

- **The draw is real.** Equal steps, equal wall: 6 → 14 exact, 9 → 22
  glyph-in-read. Nine rows per item is not worth one.
- **It is not travel.** G1500 takes the row norm 0.56 → 0.79 and glyph-in-read
  stays 22/36. The +3 exact is all seed 1, where G500 answered a one-kana
  caption with a three-glyph line in a big bubble (いだも 木才ろ スデス ポデス);
  G1500 still does (がきん ズキス パデス おきん).
- **The count habit is the grid's.** Every grid item holds 4–9 units, so
  "several glyphs" lands in the rows (the unit-count prior of `findings.md`);
  the single-caption eval and the native prompts are both out of frame for
  this table.
- **Stable wrong rows**: き → ま and か → む on seed 0 in both grid arms. One
  seed each, not yet told apart from a reader confusion (A3000 reads さ as
  き / ま) — but a wrong binding that survives 3× the steps is what smeared
  credit over k cells would look like. Needs the in-frame read below.
- **Not measured**: the table in its *own* frame. No eval renders a grid
  caption, so "did not learn" and "did not transfer to a single bubble" are
  not separated.
- 24 rows understate what a grid buys: singles already update every row once
  in 6 steps here; at 374 rows it is once in 94 against a grid's once in ≈ 15.
