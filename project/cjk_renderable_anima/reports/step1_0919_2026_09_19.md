## The table is small, and that is not the lever

`delta_norm_mean` 145 (13 k) → 75 (53 k); Δ1 did the same (161 → 79, hidden
by the table under it); plain 147 → 114; the 12-row raw ΔFM arm 133 → 121.
Rows that hit under plain sit at norm 130–230, the same rows here at 55–120.
Candidate mechanism (not measured): at 374 rows a row is drawn every ≈ 93
steps and between draws dense Adam moves it on the `--free_residual` pull
alone (the row-blocks comment in `src/train/stage.py`), and ΔFM's gradient
is ≈ 1/6 of plain's.

`--delta_scale 1.7` (median norm 76 → ≈ 130) leaves the read where it was
and adds bubbles and pseudo-text: the scale multiplies the shared component
with the identity, so the ratio between them does not move. The shortfall is
identity per unit of row, not row size.
