# band B.1 — single kana on scene composites, 0.5–0.7 vs 0.7–0.9 (2026-09-23)

`plan_band.md` Stage B rung 1: the same 24 cold kana rows trained on the same
scene-composite data at two σ bands, read on exact, native and the row's own
`cf_sense`. The cell is **scene × as-built × S**: single kana in the step 1a
bubble pool at fill 0.7 render at **48 px** (p10 37 – p90 73), not the 35 px
the plan called "small" — one glyph takes the bubble fit, and the pool has no
smaller bubble. The M pair it is read against (`micro_cf_0922`
`mcf_plain_lo` / `mcf_plain_hi`, 16 two-to-four-glyph pieces) is at 35 px.

## Setup

Rows (`chars:` base, so exact reads the first 18, native all 24):
あ い お な ぬ の ま む め ア イ ナ ネ ヌ マ ム メ ヨ や ら れ ラ ル ロ — 12 + 12,
from the rows with no voiced sibling.

```
data  … --data_tag band_s_0923 --seed 0 --scenes s1,s1w,sl1w,ja_comic --scene_one_bubble ja_comic \
        --single_scenes s1,s1w --single_max_ar 2 --units chars:<24 kana> --scene_mix single=1.0 \
        --n_items 3000 --scene_frac 1.0 --natural_frac 0 --strings_frac 0 --flat_bubble 1.0 \
        --scene_fill 0.7 --scene_min_glyph 28 --scene_max_lines 1 --scene_vertical 1 \
        --shapes 448,512:2,448x512,512x448
        → ink single: n 3000, glyph px 48 (37–73), ink/glyph cells² 9 (4–23)
train … --train_steps 4500 --batch 4 --compile 1 --lr_rows 1e-3 --lr_decay cosine --free_residual 1e-3 \
        --box_share 0.25 --pair_loss 0 --c_flat 0 --seeds 2 --no_floor --eval_groups single,en \
        --t_min 0.5 --t_max 0.7 --arm_tag bs_lo   |   --t_min 0.7 --t_max 0.9 --arm_tag bs_hi
native … --native_chars <24 rows> --native_clauses en,swap --native_limit 4 --seeds 2 --delta_parts full
cf_sense … --cf_lang ja --cf_rows single --cf_pairs 24 --cf_glyph_px 48 --cf_t 0.35,…,0.9 --eval_tag px48
```

190 steps per row (the `micro_warm_0923` budget), 37 / 36 min a train, 14
min a native. Raw pack (sha 7b9fce0b…) in every submit shell.

The high band wins 14 rows on en and loses 5 (む め ぬ ヌ ヨ, by 1–3 hits);
マ メ ロ are dead on both arms, の and イ nearly so. Katakana are weaker than
hiragana on both arms (en hits 12 vs 33 on `lo`, 27 vs 57 on `hi`).

**What the misses look like.** `bs_lo`'s exact misses are the right glyph
drawn more than once (い→いい, お→おお・, の→ののののののの, ま→ままよ);
`bs_hi`'s few misses are identity swaps (お→む, マ→チ, マ→ア, ぬ→あ). The
low band pins identity and never trains the σ where "one glyph, once" is
decided; the high band sets the count and sits above the identity peak, and
still comes out ahead on every ruler.

**The row's own leverage sits at 0.7 on both arms** (`cf_sense_ja_px48`,
24 id pairs, floor 0.00 everywhere): 0.24 / 0.25 at σ 0.7, at the EN
ceiling's magnitude for 48 px (0.27) but one step above the ceiling's peak
(0.6). `bs_lo`, trained at 0.5–0.7, reads 0.08 at 0.5 and 0.24 at 0.6–0.7;
`bs_hi` holds 0.19 at 0.8 and 0.12 at 0.9 where `bs_lo` has 0.14 / 0.05.
Leverage lands where the row was trained (Gate 0's rule), and for a single
row the useful part of it is the upper half.

## Beside the M pair (35 px pieces, same layout, same budget)

| block | glyph px | 0.5–0.7 | 0.7–0.9 | winner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M (16 pieces) | 35 | exact 7/32, native 6 / 3 of 64 | 1/32, 1 / 0 | **0.5–0.7** |
| S (24 kana) | 48 | 25/36, 49 / 30 of 192 | 29/36, 84 / 55 | **0.7–0.9** |

The two blocks pick opposite bands. Size is not the reason: the ceiling
(`cf_band_a1_2026_09_23.md`) puts a 48 px letter and a 35 px string in the
same live band (0.5–0.7 / 0.5–0.8), and in training the 48 px single went
*up*, not down. What differs is the count: a piece's glyph count is in its
token, a single's "one glyph" has to be learned at the σ where the DiT lays
out text, above 0.7. **H-count holds in training, with the sign the recipe
assumed** (singles high, pieces low), and the ceiling map does not predict a
trained single row's band — it predicts where its leverage can reach, not
which half of that the row needs.

## Consequences

- **Step 1a keeps 0.7–0.9 for singles**, at scene size (this read) and at
  grid size (the ceiling: grid cells at 85–200 px live at 0.8–0.9).
  Rung B.2 (grid × small × S) does not run — it needed a 0.5–0.7 pick —
  and B.3's question is answered at the ceiling.
- **Step 1b keeps 0.5–0.7 for pieces**; the band is keyed on the item's
  glyph count (single vs multi-glyph token), not on its rendered px,
  within the sizes step 1 trains at. The per-px table from Stage A is the
  *floor* an item's band may reach (a 16 px string lives at 0.25–0.6), not
  the band a single row trains in.
- Open, from the curriculum discussion (Kim et al. 2025, easy-to-hard =
  high σ first, lower clusters accumulated, full range last): warm from
  `bs_hi`, 40 steps per row, **switch** to 0.5–0.7 vs **accumulate** to
  0.5–0.9 — does a low-σ pass after the high band buy identity (the お→む
  swaps) without giving back the count, and does keeping the high band in
  the mix beat switching. Two arms, ≈ 25 min.
- Dead rows (マ メ ロ の イ at 0–1 of 8 on both arms) are a row question,
  not a band question; `table_geometry.py` on `bs_hi` before spending
  steps on them.
