Reads:

- **The grid is additive, not a leak.** A750 and the 50 % mix have the same
  scene draws per row; the grid's 780 draws take `single` 12 → 27 and native
  17 / 10 → 25 / 24. The plan's "M1500 below A1500 → the habit leaks" branch
  was the wrong reading of 25 vs 49: A750 is the control that says so.
- **Native is paid in scene draws.** 125 → 187 → 250 scene draws per row read
  native `en` 25 → 37 → 49 with the grid share falling 50 → 25 → 0 %. A grid
  draw is worth a fraction of a scene draw for the scene frame and about a
  full one for the flat `single` read (30/36 at 187 + 390 vs 28/36 at 250).
- **A residual count habit is there.** Seed-1 misses of the mixes are
  two-glyph answers to a one-kana caption (いっ きと くく さを のカ; M25: きた
  さきゃ のカ おきさ) where A1500's are identity misses (が て ば). Milder than
  grid-only's three-glyph lines, not gone at 25 %.

3 000 steps — under A750's 125, where native is already 17/64. The mix halves
that again. M0b therefore reads whether the grid's flat-identity gain is worth
the scene draws it displaces when both are scarce; the 25 % share is the
fallback if the 50 % mix loses native there too.
