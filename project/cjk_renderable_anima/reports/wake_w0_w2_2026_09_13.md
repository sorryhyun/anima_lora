# Wake probe report — W0 → W2 scale arms (2026-09-13)

Completed work lifted out of [`../history.md`](../history.md), which now
carries only the forward plan. Instrument:
[`../probes/wake_probe.py`](../probes/wake_probe.py); outputs under
`output/wake_probe/<stage|arm>[_<tag>]/`. Every number here is the
**largest-detector-box** readout with **both readers agreeing** (manga SFX
reader + stock PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 on the same crop) unless marked otherwise.

## The hypothesis

`plan_render` read JA-SHIP / JA-BODY's floor as "the DiT cannot render JA
from ext rows, even with a trainable body". The user's counter-reading:
Anima saw JA / KO / ZH glyphs in pretraining but the stock T5 SentencePiece
collapsed every CJK caption to `<unk>`, so it never had an *address* for them
and learned to **garble**. English works because the address (T5 pieces
spelling the word) exists. The aim moves from "teach the DiT a script" to
"wake weights": find the address, not the capacity.

Why it fit what the line already knew: findings § 9 (encoder line) — the DiT
reads near-context-free per-token codes; the text-binding probe — a 1-image
LoRA at 400 steps reproduces an exact JA line crisply (glyph drawing is cheap
for the DiT, the address never forms). No arm in the line had ever put a
pixel loss on the rows with the DiT frozen: JA-BODY had a target-stream LoRA
under a whole-crop inpaint loss where glyphs are a few percent of the pixels,
JA-SHIP had no trainable weight on the row → pixel path.

### What W0 / W1 settled

1. **The units exist.** Half of the base model's bubble text survives two
   independent readers; the "garble" is a marginal over real glyphs.
2. **Drawing needs no DiT training.** A 37-row embedding delta (≈ 150 KB,
   norm ≈ 1.1× the pack row) on a frozen DiT with a frozen adapter turns
   「Japanese text reads as "か"」 into a か. `plan_render` S6's "DiT 본체 LoRA"
   was aimed at a capacity that was never missing. **Never spend a body LoRA
   on glyph shape.**
3. **s21 / arm R / s13 ("rows are addresses, content inert") were measured
   on a DiT that could not read the rows.** They stand for the recipes they
   measured, not for what a row *can* carry.
4. **Rows-only at 3e-3 (row-norm units) walks off the manifold** (2.4× row
   norm, no loss payoff). Keep rows ≤ 1e-3.
5. **Sequences do not compose**: 2–3-char strings render one glyph (ああい→あ,
   えくく→くくく); 200 training combos did not teach order.

## Geometry of the trained address (CPU/GPU probe, same day)

`rows_few/trained.pt` against the pack rows and the stock
`net.llm_adapter.embed.weight` (32128 × 1024); adapter outputs from Qwen +
the 6-block `llm_adapter` on the clause template, no DiT.

| quantity | rows_few delta | comparison |
|---|---|---|
| pairwise cosine among row deltas | 0.04 | pack rows 0.25 |
| participation ratio | 25 of 37 | near full rank |
| cosine to own pack row | −0.05 | orthogonal to the init |
| energy in top-256 stock T5 PCs | 0.31 | gaussian 0.25, real T5 rows 0.45 |
| shared component (cos to mean delta) | 0.25 | a weak "one big kana" mode |

At the adapter output (ext position): the delta rewrites the code
(Δ/‖out‖ ≈ 1.3, cos(before, after) 0.2–0.3); the 8 trained codes are more
spread than pack codes (pairwise cos 0.28 vs 0.49), PR 6.5/8; corr(row-cos,
output-cos) 0.73 → the adapter is roughly linear on the address; plain vs
bubble template output cos 0.99 → **context-free**; trained codes sit near
nothing (max cos to any kana/Latin token output ≤ 0.38). **Leak:** an ext-row
delta moves the *other* clause tokens' adapter outputs by up to ~40 % of
their norm through the adapter's self-attention (EN-only prompts unaffected —
no ext id).

Verdict: no low-dimensional manifold to fit without the DiT. The row → glyph
map lives only in the DiT's cross-attention. A manifold can only be
*constructed* (an amortized glyph → row encoder), not found.

## W2 scale arms

### 256² — MISS; resolution is a dead cost lever

`rows_w2` (train `-ce20a0`, eval `-2f1221` / `-ae3d77`): 92 kana, rows only,
`--n_single 40` (4859 items, 76 % singles), **256²**, batch 16, lr 1e-3,
2200 steps = W1's per-row exposure (~290 singles/kana) at 4× W1's samples/s
(1.04 it/s, 8.7 GB with grad-ckpt, 36 min). Delta 0.98× row norm.

| eval | single exact | combo | corpus | EN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 512² | **1/36** (は, seed 0) | 0/36, CER 0.99 | 0/20 | 24/24 |
| 256² | 0/36 | 0/36 | 0/20 | **11/24** |

At 512² the delta moves the layout (about half the seed-0 prompts render one
big clean glyph — ぬ→「めよ」, テ→「ジャト」, ン→「ンス」) but the identity is
wrong. At 256² even the untouched EN control falls to 11/24: **the base model
cannot spell at 256², so a 256² pixel loss carries no glyph-identity
gradient**; the arm trained layout only. Never train or evaluate glyph rows
below 512². Mixing resolutions would only dilute the 512² signal.

### 24 kana at 512² — PARTIAL; rows interfere, exposure alone does not scale

`rows_w24` (train `-752d01`, eval `-5e3d1e`): あ…ね (first 24 hiragana), rows
only, `--n_single 40` (1776 items, 54 % singles), 512², batch 4, lr 1e-3,
3200 steps ≈ 290 singles/kana. Delta 0.99× row norm.

| arm | kana | single exact | seed 0 | seed 1 | combo CER | EN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 `rows_few` | 8 | 8/16 (50 %) | 6/8 | 2/8 | 0.70 | 24/24 |
| `rows_w24` | 24 | **10/36 (28 %)** | 7/18 | 3/18 | 0.78 | 24/24 |

Hits: あ え お こ さ た ち (seed 0), え た ち (seed 1). か き く rendered at 8
kana and do not at 24 at matched exposure → **rows interfere**; the FM loss
never asks rows to differ. Combos render one glyph and it is the *strongest
single* of the string, not the first (くお→お, こえ→え, すこた→た, とけ→と): the
addresses compete inside the clause, consistent with the adapter self-attn
leak. Composition levers are therefore discrimination levers too.

### 1024² transfer of the 24-kana delta (user's ask, `eval_1024/`, job `-6bbce6`)

`--eval_size 1024 --seeds 1 --eval_groups single,combo --eval_limit 8`, 16
images, 4.4 min. Singles **3/8 both readers: あ え お** — exactly the
characters that hit at 512² seed 0 — drawn as one large font-clean glyph
in a bubble or on the plain canvas. The address transfers to 1024² for
every character it discriminates. Characters that failed at 512² fail
differently at 1024²: the manga prior at that size fills the bubble with a
long salad *sentence* (い, か, き), so identity failure reads as salad, not as
a wrong single glyph. Combos: 「ううさ」→「ううか」 (first near-hit on a 3-char
string), 「くねさ」→「さ」, 「こお」→「お」 — strongest-single again.
`sheet_single.png` there is the one to look at.

### W2a balanced batches — MISS

`rows_w24bal` (data `--balanced 4`, train `-839501`, eval `-28f69f`): the w24
recipe (same inventory, 960 singles / 700 combos / 116 corpus, byte-identical
`eval.json`), but every batch is 4 distinct strings rendered in one layout
(font, canvas, bubble, glyph size/position). Corpus crops go in plain shuffled
groups of 4. 3200 steps, 23.1 min, 2.30 it/s; delta 0.98× row norm (w24 0.99×);
late loss 0.042 (w24 0.038).

| arm | single exact (both) | sfx | vl | seed 0 | seed 1 | combo CER sfx/vl | EN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rows_w24` | 10/36 | 10 | 12 | 7/18 | 3/18 | 0.78 / 0.78 | 24/24 |
| `rows_w24bal` | **6/36** | 7 | 6 | 4/18 | 2/18 | 0.82 / 0.84 | 24/24 |

Hits: あ え き ち (seed 0), あ こ (seed 1). On the seed-0 sheet almost every
single renders as one clean, font-like glyph (layout solved), but identity is
often wrong, and え is an attractor (い, さ, つ → え; か → わ, け → は). く こ し
read correctly on the SFX reader only. Mechanism: with free rows and a
batch-mean loss, a row's gradient is the sum over its own items, so batch
composition cannot cancel layout. The balance has to live in a loss that
compares items (W2c swap-contrastive, where the balanced groups give a
same-layout wrong caption for free).

### Classifier diagnostic — identity lives at σ ≈ 0.8

`--stage classify` on `rows_w24` (job `-0fe267`, 20 min at 13.5 fwd/s, batch
24): 48 held-out single renders (2 per kana, own rng stream), each noised once
per σ and scored under all 24 kana captions in its own template. Output:
`rows_w24/classify/{classify.md,classify.pt}`.

| σ | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.35 | 0.50 | 0.65 | **0.80** | 0.95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| trained top-1 (/48) | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 8 | **19** | 1 |
| trained gap/spread | −0.07 | 0.00 | 0.17 | 0.34 | 0.32 | **1.67** | −0.09 |
| floor top-1 (/48) | 5 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 4 | 4 |

At σ 0.8: top-3 33/48, 16 of 24 kana hit at least once (renders: 7), bubble
14/33 vs plain 5/15. Most-picked wrong answers there: つ ×7, う ×5, き ×5. Summing errors
over σ is a bad pooling: σ 0.95's spread (340) buries the band, and the pooled
top-1 falls to 8/48 with つ as the attractor. The default sigmoid timestep
sampling puts 18.5 % of training steps in σ 0.7–0.9 and 66 % at σ ≤ 0.6.
Readings: (1) the rows carry identity in the loss beyond what the renders
show, so part of the loss is on the sampling side, but 60 % are still wrong
at the best σ, so the rows are the larger limit; (2) the plan's `--t_max 0.6`
σ restriction pointed the wrong way; (3) a CE term belongs in σ 0.7–0.9 with
τ ≈ 160 summed-error units.

### Native rendering — the address is template-bound and carries a layout prior

`--stage native` (job `-bef9ac`, 4 min): the 8 blind-pairs scene prompts
(`project/cjk_aware_anima/assets/unmask_eval_prompts.txt`) with a kana clause
appended, `rows_w24_band` delta on, あ / か, 2 seeds, 512², no floor. Sheets
under `rows_w24_band/native/`.

| clause | n | hit sfx | hit vl | both | trained rows / caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `…, japanese text. Japanese text reads as "か".` (trained shape) | 32 | 16 | 13 | 13 | 1.0 of 1 ext row |
| `…. ひらがなの「か」という文字がある。` (JA sentence) | 32 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3.0 of 11 ext rows |

- **Trained clause shape: the glyph comes, the scene often goes.** The row
  brings its training layout with it (the shared "one big kana on a blank
  canvas" direction of the geometry probe): か seed 0 wiped the scene on 7/8
  prompts (big か on white/grey); `simple background` (p06) is always a bare
  glyph. When the scene survives (あ more often than か; seed 1 more than 0)
  the kana sits in a caption strip at the bottom inside a salad string
  (「ばにしんあ」「あでン」「か簾毛代季か」), so the both-reader hit (13/32)
  undercounts glyphs that are visibly there.
- **JA sentence clause: dead (1/32).** Its own words route to 11 ext rows, 3
  of them trained (と い う from という / あ), so the caption carries several
  competing addresses and none of the template the rows were trained inside;
  output is scene + salad or no text at all. The address is *only* the piece
  inside `Japanese text reads as "…"`.
- Consequence: product captions keep the trained clause grammar verbatim, and
  the layout prior needs scene-embedded training renders (glyph pasted into a
  picture, caption = picture caption + clause) so the row cannot explain the
  canvas.

### Kanji probe (`rows_k24_band`) — atoms render, composites decompose

Same recipe as `rows_w24_band` on 24 kanji chosen for structure, not
frequency: 12 IDS atoms (日 月 木 人 口 女 子 田 力 山 石 目) + 12 composites
built only from them (明 林 森 休 好 男 岩 品 加 晶 相 困). Each is one Qwen
piece = one ext row. `--only_chars … --n_corpus 0` (corpus crops are
kana-only), 844 font items, train `-ed7905` (23 min; delta ends at 1.39×
row norm vs 1.02× for kana — composites 1.82, atoms 1.53), eval singles =
atoms + 明 林 森 休 好 男.

| arm | singles sfx exact | both readers | combo exact | EN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rows_w24_band` (kana) | 31/36 | 24/36 | 0/36 | 24/24 |
| `rows_k24_band` (kanji) | 12/36 | 11/36 | 1/36 | 24/24 |

- **Atoms render.** Seed 0 draws 日 月 木 人 女 子 田 力 石 目 correctly (10/12;
  口 → salad, 山 → 山 + salad); readers undercount (力 read as カ, 石 as 丕).
  Kanji units exist in the frozen DiT at the exposure that gave kana 24/36 —
  harder, not dead.
- **Composites decompose instead of merging.** 林 → a single 木, 森 → 「木林」
  as two glyphs, 明 / 休 → salad; 好 and 男 render. The composite row
  evokes its *parts* and the DiT lays them out as separate characters.
  Combos show the same thing from the other side — several rows *merge*
  into one character-like glyph: 品休 → 和, 相晶加 → 朋, 林月力 → 初, 男休 →
  甥, 好力月 → 刀 over 月, 木木石 → three glyphs, 山人 → 山人 exact. With kana
  a multi-row caption rendered the first glyph; with kanji the DiT composes
  at the **component** level, in pixels. The glyph manifold lives in the
  DiT's cross-attention, not in row space.
- **Row geometry (`probes/wake_geometry.py`).** Pairwise cos 0.26 raw
  (shared mode, cos-to-mean 0.53 — stronger than `rows_few`'s 0.25),
  −0.04 mean-removed. cos(Δ_composite, ΣΔ_atoms) against sums of random
  rows: 男 98th pct, 相 94, 困 94, 休 87 (mean-removed 99 / 89 / 95 / 86;
  林 森 晶 83–85), but 明 26, 好 8, 品 0, 加 16, 岩 49. Four of twelve ≥ 94th
  pct where chance gives < 1 — weak additive structure for the side-by-side
  composites, none for stacked / tripled ones. Not a manifold an encoder
  could read off the rows alone, consistent with the kana verdict: it has to
  be constructed through the DiT.

### What W2 settled (the case for W2d)

- Free rows are per-character inversions: identity lives at σ ≈ 0.8, the σ
  band lifts singles, but every character costs its own exposure and dense
  glyphs cost more. Batch balancing cannot help (rows are free parameters;
  the FM loss is a batch mean).
- The address is bit-exact on ext-free prompts (EN 24/24 on every arm), so
  anything whose *output* is an ext-row delta keeps the base model intact.
  An encoder that emits row deltas inherits that; a DiT- or adapter-side LoRA
  does not.
- Composition is not a rows problem: kana combos render the first glyph,
  kanji combos fuse into one glyph. Token → position binding was never
  learned by the DiT (pretraining saw `<unk>` runs) and needs DiT-side,
  ext-gated work (W3).
- The layout prior is a data artefact (blank canvases) and ships with the
  rows; scene-composite renders are required before anything is packed.

## W2d — amortised glyph encoder, run 1 (kana, held-out 32; same night)

`--arm encoder --data_tag w2 --held_out 32`, 92 kana at 512², σ band
0.7–0.9, batch 4, compile, no ckpt. Ten launches; the first seven died on
the train log and each one fixed the parametrisation, not the idea
(attempt table + per-attempt logs: `history.md` and
`output/wake_probe/encoder_w2_held32/train_log_attempt*.json`).

| lever | what it fixed | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| centre the identity, layout mode as one vector `c` bounded on the parameter (cap 0.75) | common-mode runaway (18–36× row norm) | `c` pins at the cap by step 300 in every run, rows bounded |
| lr 3e-5 on the CNN (from 3e-4) | Adam's per-step kick — spread swung 0.005 ↔ 0.4 and collapsed to ~0 every ~100 steps, never accumulating | monotone growth from the first log at 3e-5 |
| spatial flatten (6×6×256 → 256) on a **font-free mean render** | output tracked font ~8× more than glyph with a global mean pool (same-font spread flat at 0.1 while the random-font draw climbed to 1.05) | same-font spread == training spread, 0.2 → 2.3× |
| cosine decay to 0 | linear identity growth with no bound (1.6× at 2000, 2.5× at 2125) | plateau at 2.32× by step 5000 |

Two renders, both seeds, both readers, largest box (`report.md` is
min-over-boxes):

| run | trained singles | held-out singles | combo | corpus | EN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2000 steps (`_s2k`) | 4/24 | 1/64 | 0/36 | 0/20 | 24/24 |
| 6000 steps cosine (`_s6k_cos`) | **6/24** | **3/64** | 0/36 | 0/20 | 24/24 |
| free rows, σ band (reference) | 24/36 | — | — | — | 24/24 |

**What the sheets say.** Every render, trained or held-out, is a crisp
single kana on a blank canvas — the identity is signal, not Adam drift,
and `c` carries the layout mode as intended. At 2000 steps the map
collapses onto ~8 attractor kana; at 6000 it resolves further (ヲ ヌ Z フ
ね を ス appear) and the misses are shape-neighbours at finer grain:
trained ち/る, ひ/い, リ/ソ, チ/テ decided by the seed; held-out ま→も,
れ→わ, ケ→チ, ア→テ, ネ→テ, コ→フ, ク→ン, け→ね, ふ→ん, ワ/レ→シ. Exact
held-out hits: テ (one seed), く (both).

**Verdict.** The glyph → row map exists and generalises **by shape** to
glyphs that never received a gradient — the thing free rows cannot do —
but at this capacity / objective it cannot separate neighbours, so the
trained gate (≥ 67 %) fails at 25 % while the held-out gate (> 0) passes
weakly. The pre-registered kill rule fires on its letter ("< 50 % after one
retune") and misses on its premise (it assumed the encoder could not
memorise; it memorises at the wrong granularity). Levers spent: optimizer
(lr, decay), input (pool, font). Lever left: the loss — neighbour
separation, i.e. the same-noise contrastive term shelved for free rows
because it cannot fix combos, which is not W2d's job. The decision between
that run and the glyph-image fallback is the user's; `history.md`
carries both.

## Instruments and gotchas (as learned)

- `probes/wake_probe.py --stage salad|data|train|eval --arm rows|rows_adapter
  [--data_tag T --only_chars … --n_single N]`; train flags `--train_size
  --batch --lr_rows --train_steps --grad_ckpt --compile --aggressive_recompute
  --activation_memory_budget --t_min --t_max`; eval flags `--eval_size
  --seeds --no_floor --eval_tag T` (writes `<arm>/eval_T/`) `--eval_groups
  single,combo --eval_limit N`. Data stage is CPU-only and safe inline; every
  GPU stage via `make daemon-run … --queue` + `make daemon-wait`.
- **Read the largest detector box, not the whole image**; `report.md` is the
  min-over-boxes score — recompute from `eval_reads.json`.
- Ext rows are **Qwen-piece** keyed: 「いいよ」 is two rows (いい + よ).
- The VAE takes `IMAGE_TRANSFORMS` range ([-1, 1]).
