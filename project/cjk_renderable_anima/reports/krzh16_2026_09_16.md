# krzh16 — 16 Korean / simplified-Chinese rows on the micro recipe, read against the 53k table (2026-09-16)

> **Question (user):** train KO / ZH glyph rows the way the kana singles were
> trained and see where they land relative to the kana ext rows. **Arm:** 8
> Hangul (가 힝 ㄹ 몹 감 없 양 한) + 8 simplified hanzi (你 这 个 们 说 国 时 为),
> none in the 53k table, rows recipe of record (frame-mix composites 0.9 on
> s0+s1, flat bubble 0.1, fill 0.7, min glyph 32, Q off, `c_flat` 0), 4 000
> items, 4 000 steps ≈ 1 000 draws per row, 34 min. **Result:** the DiT
> holds Hangul and simplified units — Hangul renders as Hangul (감 exact by
> the VL reader, 몹 → 몸, 가 → 기, jamo-level near misses), hanzi 5/16 exact
> by the SFX reader (你 2/2). **Geometry:** the rows rebuild the same shared
> trigger as the 53k table (cos to m̂ +0.41 vs +0.46), carry kanji-sized
> norms (0.80), and their identity sits *outside* the kana shared subspace
> (0.17 of energy in the 53k top-40 vs 0.45 for 53k rows). Nearest 53k
> neighbours are radical / shape neighbours at cos 0.3–0.4 (时 → 時, 你 →
> 他, 们 → 付/仕, 这 → 遠/達, 国 → 開/員), never above the 0.5–0.57 a row's own
> character reaches when the same table is retrained. Concatenating such a
> table onto the 53k table is mechanically sound (disjoint ids, per-row
> additive hook) with two fixes: rescale `raw` across the two `row_scale`s
> (196.4 vs 190.9) and drop the one overlapping row, **58974 = the `~`
> symbol row**, which both tables train through the `kaguya-sama … ~tensai
> …~` copyright tag (the 53k "norm 2.2 clause piece" is this row, not a
> clause piece).

Reading: the KO / ZH rows sit on the same manifold as the kana ext rows —
the shared trigger plus a near-orthogonal identity — and their identity lies
outside the kana rows' shared subspace as much as any 53k row's does.
Hanzi connect to JA rows at the radical level (亻 辶 囗, simplified ↔
traditional); Hangul connects only at shape-neighbour strength and to no
particular family. Hangul rows are as large as kanji rows, not kana rows.

## The 53k table's "norm 2.2 shared row" is `~`

Both tables contain ext row **58974**, the pack's symbol row for `~`
(`sym["93"]`), trained through the 32 / 81 composites whose copyright tag is
`kaguya-sama wa kokurasetai ~tensai-tachi no renai zunousen~`. In the 53k
table it reached norm 2.2 (`plan_synth.md` called it "the clause's common
piece"); here 0.26; the two vectors are unrelated (cos −0.03). It is a tag
leak, not a clause piece: a shipped table must drop it, and a scene pool
should strip `~` from tags (or the tokenizer's symbol route should skip it
inside tag names).

## Concatenating tables (feasibility)

Mechanically sound: `ExtDelta` is per-row additive with no cross-row
normalisation, so a caption that touches only KO rows renders identically
whether or not the 433 JA rows are loaded; the bake is `ext_embed[r] +=
raw[i] · row_scale` per row. Two fixes for a merge utility: (1) `raw` is in
each table's own row-norm units (`row_scale` 196.4 vs 190.9) — rescale one
table by the ratio before concatenating; (2) resolve the `~` overlap by
dropping it. Open questions before shipping such a table: the KO rows were
trained under the JA caption frame and rows are frame-bound (か 0/16 under
`swap`), so a deploy-facing KO table wants `Korean text reads as` in its
data; and G2 (the pack's KO / ZH tag grid, old vs baked) has not been run
for any table yet.

## Verdicts

- The DiT holds Hangul and simplified-hanzi units; the rows recipe reaches
  jamo / component-level identity at ≈ 1 000 draws per row, same as kana.
- KO / ZH rows share the kana rows' manifold (trigger + orthogonal identity);
  hanzi neighbour JA rows by radical, Hangul by shape only.
- Row addresses are reproducible across independent runs (same character
  0.5, shape neighbour 0.3, random 0.03) — the ruler for any future
  "does X land near Y" question.
- `~` is a tag leak in every scene-composite table; drop the row at bake.
