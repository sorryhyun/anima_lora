T-LoRA

Timestep-dependent rank masking. The effective LoRA rank varies with the denoising timestep via a power-law schedule:
• High noise (early steps) → reduced rank (down to min_rank)
• Low noise (late steps) → full rank (maximum expressiveness)
Withholds capacity where structure — layout, pose, identity — is being decided, since that is where a fine-tune memorizes; full rank opens up at the clean end, where style detail is learned. The mask is training-only — inference uses full rank at every t by design — so the saved .safetensors is a regular bakeable LoRA. See docs/methods/timestep_mask.md.
