Turbo (DP-DMD)

Few-step distillation via Diversity-Preserved DMD (Wu et al., arXiv:2602.03139). Distills the 28-step CFG=4 Anima teacher into a 4-step student, with three roles — student, fake-score, and teacher — sharing one frozen DiT. CFG is baked into the student during distillation.

The output is a plain standard LoRA. There is no inference-side turbo code: you load it through the normal LoRA adapter path and run --infer_steps 4 --cfg 1.0 (make test-turbo). It composes with concept LoRAs the way LCM-LoRA composes with style LoRAs (linear composition, ranks add), and is fully bakeable into the DiT.

A ready-made 4-step student is published at huggingface.co/sorryhyun/anima-turbo-4step — download it and point --lora_weight at it to try few-step inference without distilling your own.

Training runs through scripts/distill_turbo.py — a bespoke single-GPU loop, not train.py. Launch with make turbo (honors PRESET; append overrides via ARGS=…).

Config is the odd one out. configs/methods/turbo.toml is a bespoke sectioned schema read only by the script — the flat method+preset merge does not apply, so don't print-config METHOD=turbo. CLI flags override TOML values. See docs/methods/turbo.md (ops) and docs/structure/turbo.md (math).
